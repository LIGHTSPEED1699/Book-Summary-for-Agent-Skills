The Servomechanism

A servomechanism is a closed loop: it measures its own output, compares it to a desired setpoint, and drives the actuator to reduce the error. Wiener's cybernetics (from Greek kubernētēs, "steersman") is precisely the study of such feedback systems.

Why Lags Destabilize

Every integrator contributes 90° of phase lag. Feedback is stabilizing only while it stays negative. Once the loop's phase reaches −180°, the feedback flips to positive and the loop regenerates its own error — the onset of oscillation.

The Trade-off

More integrators and higher gain make the loop responsive — it tracks faster and rejects noise better. But they consume the stability margin. The Nyquist plot shows the moment the loop crosses the critical −1 point.
