# Glossary — CSA B149.3

**AHJ (Authority Having Jurisdiction)** — the regulator who grants field approvals; decides simple vs complex documentation and may adopt informative annexes.

**ALARP** — "as low as reasonably practicable" risk criterion (Annex I): further risk reduction is required until cost becomes grossly disproportionate to safety gained.

**BMS (Burner Management System)** — control system supervising prepurge, ignition, flame monitoring, and safe shutdown; may be hardwired or microprocessor-based (Clause 12.7).

**C/I (Close-and-Interlock)** — marking on a Z21.21/CSA 6.5 valve indicating manual-reset capability; substitutes for a second SSOV in mid-tier trains.

**Field approval** — AHJ approval of an appliance assembled/modified in the field whose fuel-related components are not factory-certified as a unit; the core purpose of B149.3.

**FARC** — electronic fuel-air ratio control; certified per ISO 23552-1 or engineered with cross-limiting and microprocessor supervision (Annex D).

**Flame safeguard** — control proving flame presence and shutting fuel on loss; response typically ≤ 4 s.

**LFL / UFL** — lower/upper flammable limit; hydrogen LFL 4.0%, UFL 75.0% (Annex N); LFL drives Class A oven ventilation (Clause 16.10).

**Lock-up regulator** — positive shut-off pressure regulator, required when inlet exceeds 0.5 psig (Clause 4.6).

**Low fire start** — burner ignition at minimum firing rate; mandatory above 3.5 MMBtu/h (Clause 12.3).

**LP gas** — liquefied petroleum gas (propane/butane); valve trains require Schedule 80 pipe, Class 300 fittings (Clause 9).

**Prepurge** — timed air exchange before ignition; 4 air changes of combustion chamber + flue passages (Clause 12.2).

**Proof of closure switch** — device proving an SSOV is fully closed before the start sequence proceeds; required on progressively more valves as input increases (Clause 8).

**Pilot turndown test** — Annex A test finding the minimum pilot heat release that reliably lights the main burner; 3 successful attempts.

**QMS (Quality Management System)** — documented quality program required by Annex I's risk-based approach for complex facilities.

**Rating plate** — mandatory plate listing assembler, model, input, fuel, pressures, electrical data (Clause 14).

**SSOV (Safety shut-off valve)** — automatic valve certified to close on de-energization; certified to Z21.21/CSA 6.5 or CGA 3.9 (industrial), or combination controls (Z21.78/6.20) at low input.

**Test firing valve** — hand valve downstream of SSOVs for controlled main-burner ignition attempts; end-switch interlock required above 12.5 MMBtu/h (Clause 6.6).

**Trial for ignition** — timed period to establish flame: ≤ 10 s for pilots (11.3.2) and main flames (11.2.3).

**Valve train** — assembled components between appliance manual shut-off valve and burner: regulator, SSOVs, vents, gauges, safety devices.

**VPS (Valve proving system)** — programming unit + measuring device + valves proving SSOV tightness each cycle (Annex E); at ≥ 12.5 MMBtu/h the main SSOVs shall be VPS-supervised or have an automatic vent valve (8.1.12). An alternative to double block and bleed (E.2.1) — not a substitute for proof of closure.

**Zones 0/1/2, 20/21/22** — CEC hazardous-location zones by gas/dust presence frequency (Annex L).

**6 air changes per hour** — minimum ventilation (or area classification study) for H2 blends > 25% above 66 psi (Annex N).
