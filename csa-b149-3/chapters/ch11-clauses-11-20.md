# Chapter 11 — Clauses 11–20: Ignition, Controls, Electrical, Process Appliances, and Special Equipment

## Overview
Clauses 11–20 cover the dynamic safety side: ignition sequencing, safety controls, electrical, rating plates, start-up, process appliances (ovens/furnaces/incinerators), engines and turbines, flare pilots, complex facilities, and portable equipment.

## Core Content
### Clause 11 — Ignition
- **11.2.3** Main flame trial-for-ignition: ≤ **10 s** (interval between main SSOV opening and pilot closure where applicable).
- **11.3.2** Pilot trial-for-ignition: ≤ **10 s** maximum spark/pilot trial.
- Ignition energy must light the burner reliably at minimum firing rate; pilots proven before main fuel (except direct spark ignition).

### Clause 12 — Safety controls and devices
- **12.1 Flame safeguard**: response time from flame loss to fuel shut-off — pilot 4 s typical; main burner per standard; flame signal verified each cycle.
- **12.2 Prepurge**: ≥ **4 air changes** of combustion chamber + flue passages before ignition (high-input equipment; airflow ≥ 80% of max input rate during purge).
- **12.3** Low fire start: burners > **3.5 MMBtu/h** start at low fire; modulation only after stable low-fire proof.
- **12.5 Temperature/pressure limits**: high gas pressure device set per 12.5.1 (typically not above max component rating); low gas pressure per 12.5.2 (typically ~50% of normal); temperature limits per 12.5.4.
- **12.6** Low gas pressure safety device set at ~50% of normal outlet pressure; airflow proving (12.1.x) via differential switch, and loss of airflow must trip fuel.
- **12.7** Microprocessor-based/BMS controls: certified or engineered with self-diagnostics, watchdog, safe-state on fault; programmable controllers follow the same functional requirements as hardwired safeties.

### Clause 13 — Electrical
- Field assemblies wired per CEC Part I (C22.1:24); safety circuits failsafe; electrical area classification documented.

### Clause 14 — Rating plate
- Appliance must bear a rating plate: manufacturer/assembler, model, input rating, fuel type, manifold pressure, electrical ratings, and field-approval identification.

### Clause 15 — Initial start-up
- Start-up by competent personnel; verify all safety interlocks; high-input equipment follows Annex A procedure (dry-run cycles + pilot turndown test).

### Clause 16 — Process appliances (ovens, furnaces, incinerators, process heaters)
- **16.1 Scope/NAICS exemptions**: upstream oil & gas (21111), refineries (32411), petrochemical (32511), fertilizer/pesticide, inorganic/organic chemical (32531/32519), and similar — largely exempt where process-specific regulation governs.
- **16.2 Fire hazards**: atmosphere temperatures ≥ 1400 °F (760 °C) special rules; no negative-pressure operation; alarm and interlock on purge-gas loss; clearance to combustibles limited to 194 °F (90 °C) surface temperature; combustible floors need air channels; **explosion relief** required (16.2.4) where deflagration risk exists.
- **16.3 Safety devices**: manual reset on each safety device; flame supervision 4 s; line-burner pilots may serve burner groups; premix pairs; thermocouple controls ≤ 400k Btu/h.
- **16.7 Radiant tubes** (16.7.4–16.9): tube integrity, negative pressure within tubes, flame detection per burner or group.
- **16.9 Atmosphere generators** (endothermic/exothermic): generator purge, dew-point control, non-reactive atmosphere interlocks.
- **16.10 Class A ovens**: solvent-laden ventilation — LFL-based minimum ventilation rate calculation considering oven size, solvent amount/type, temperature, altitude; continuous and batch ovens, powder-coat ovens; interlocks tie exhaust to burner operation.

### Clause 17 — Generators, compressors/pressure boosters, engines, turbines
- **17.1** Defers to B149.1 Clause 7.2 for installation.
- **17.2.1** Compressors/boosters: **low pressure cut-off** required to shut off fuel when suction pressure falls below the safe minimum (protects engine/fuel-side integrity).

### Clause 18 — Flare pilots
- **18.1.3** Remote flame-front generators **prohibited** for flare pilot ignition (must ignite at the tip).
- **18.1.4** Performance: flare pilot must light and remain lit under worst-case wind/effluent conditions.
- **18.3** Fuel conditioning device (strainer/filter/knockout) upstream of the flare-pilot regulator, with indication of plugging.
- **18.7** Installation per B149.1 + design/installation/operation/maintenance records to owner.
- **18.8 Continuous operation**: dedicated automatic ignition system; dedicated flame detection distinguishing pilot vs flare tip flames (thermocouple, ionization, optical, acoustic); ignition continuous or on flame-loss; **alarm within 15 min** of flame loss, failsafe-wired.
- **18.9 Management systems**: documented procedures, event logs, training, inspection/maintenance; records retained **5 years** (or per AHJ).

### Clause 19 — Complex and integrated facilities
- Appliances in complex/integrated facilities may follow the **risk-based program of Annex I** (QMS, team competence, hazard scenarios, risk matrix, ALARP criteria) to determine component requirements, where legislation permits audit of inspection/maintenance.

### Clause 20 — Portable appliances
- Portable/relocatable gas equipment per B149.6; securement, quick-disconnects, overpressure protection suited to transport.

## Key Takeaways
- Trial-for-ignition ≤ 10 s for pilots and main flames; flame loss to fuel shut-off ~4 s.
- 4 air changes prepurge and low-fire start above 3.5 MMBtu/h are the two most-cited Clause 12 rules.
- Process appliances add explosion relief, 1400 °F rules, and LFL-based ventilation for Class A ovens.
- Flare pilots: ignition at the tip only (no remote flame front), conditioned fuel, 15-minute alarm, 5-year records.
- Complex facilities can trade prescriptive hardware for Annex I's audited risk-based program.
