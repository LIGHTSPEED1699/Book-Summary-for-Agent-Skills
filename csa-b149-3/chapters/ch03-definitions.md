# Chapter 3 — Definitions (Clause 3)

## Overview
Clause 3 defines the terms that give B149.3 its precise meaning. Field approval disputes usually come back to these definitions — especially *field approval*, *valve train*, *VPS*, *complex appliance*, and *ignition source types*.

## Core Content
### Terms that shape scope and approval
- **Field approval** — approval granted by the AHJ for an appliance/equipment whose fuel-related components are not certified as a unit; the AHJ reviews documentation and inspects the assembly.
- **Valve train** — the assembled piping and components between the appliance manual shut-off valve and the burner(s): regulators, SSOVs, vent valves, flow controls, gauges, safety devices.
- **Complex appliance** — an appliance whose assembly, firing controls, or integration require engineering evaluation beyond simple certification (drives enhanced documentation under Clause 10 and Annex M).
- **Appliance input rating** — maximum fuel input in Btu/h (kW); the tier-selection variable for nearly every valve/control table.

### Control and safety terms
- **SSOV (safety shut-off valve)** — automatic valve certified to close on de-energization; opens only when energized.
- **Proof of closure switch** — proves SSOV is fully closed before start sequence permits prepurge/ignition.
- **VPS (valve proving system)** — programming unit + measuring device + valves that prove SSOV tightness each cycle (Annex E).
- **Combustion safety control / flame safeguard** — the control that supervises prepurge, ignition trial, flame presence, and safe shutdown (certified per Z21.20/C22.2 60730-2-5, CAN1-6.4).
- **Manual reset / close-and-interlock ("C/I")** — valve or control that must be manually reset after trip; cannot auto-restart.
- **Ignition source types** — standing pilot, intermittent pilot, direct spark, hot surface, and other listed types (Clause 3 lists modes of pilot operation: continuous, intermittent, interrupted).
- **Prepurge** — timed air exchange of the combustion chamber (Clause 12.2 requires 4 air changes for many appliances).
- **Flame safeguard response time** — maximum time from flame loss to main fuel shut-off (Clause 12.1).

### Fuel and system terms
- **FARC** — electronic fuel-air ratio control (Annex D).
- **Zero governor / gas-air proportioning** — regulator types with special venting rules (Clause 4.8).
- **Lighter-than-air / heavier-than-air gas** — determines pilot regulator bleed-vent routing (Clause 4.7).
- **Flammable limits (LFL/UFL)** — LFL 4.0% / UFL 75.0% for hydrogen (Annex N); LFL drives oven ventilation math (Clause 16.10).
- **ALARP** — as low as reasonably practicable; risk criterion in Annex I.
- **Ordinary vs hazardous location** — electrical classification terms from CEC (Annex L).

## Key Takeaways
- "Field approval" is an AHJ-granted status, not a self-declaration — the definitions anchor who does what.
- Input rating tiers (20k, 400k, 3.5 MMBtu/h, 5 MMBtu/h, 12.5 MMBtu/h Btu/h thresholds) originate here and cascade through Clauses 6–8.
- Proof of closure and C/I certification are the two mechanisms the code uses to credit a single SSOV where two would otherwise be required (8.1.4/8.1.5). VPS is a leakage-supervision layer (8.1.12), not a valve substitute.
- Definitions in Annexes I and N (ALARP, infallible containment, flammable limits) are annex-specific — read them with their annex.
