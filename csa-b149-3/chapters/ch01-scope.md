# Chapter 1 — Scope (Clause 1)

## Overview
CSA B149.3:25 is the *Code for the field approval of fuel-related components on appliances and equipment that use gaseous or liquid fuels*. It defines the minimum safety requirements for assembling fuel-related components (valve trains, controls, pilots, burners) on appliances assembled or modified in the field, where those appliances are not certified as complete units. The Code applies downstream of the manual shut-off valve required by CSA B149.1 Clause 6.18.2.

## Core Content
- **Purpose**: Establish requirements for field approval — the AHJ (authority having jurisdiction) reviews and approves appliances whose fuel-related components are assembled on site rather than certified at a factory.
- **Applies to**:
  - Gaseous fuel appliances (natural gas, propane, LP gas) and equipment with field-assembled fuel components.
  - New assemblies and relocations/alterations of existing installations where components change.
  - Both indoor and outdoor installations.
- **Key boundary**: The Code covers the *fuel-related components and their assembly* — from the manual shut-off valve downstream to the burner. Appliance heat exchangers, pressure vessels, and venting follow other codes (B149.1, B149.2, ASME, etc.).
- **Exclusions (typical)**: Appliances certified to a recognized standard as complete units; residential appliances covered fully by other B149 codes; equipment covered by Clause 16 NAICS exemptions (upstream oil and gas, refining, petrochemical, fertilizer, pesticide, and similar process industries); portable appliances unless specifically addressed (see Clause 20 and Annex J).
- **Units**: Requirements are stated in both SI and imperial units; Btu/h and kW are given for all input ratings.
- **Compliance approach**: Field approval documentation ( Clause 10) drives what the AHJ needs — P&IDs, BOMs, wiring diagrams, control narratives, commissioning reports.

## Decision Matrix — Which Code Applies
| Situation | Governing requirement |
|---|---|
| Complete certified appliance | Product standard + B149.1 installation |
| Field-assembled valve train on gas appliance | B149.3 field approval |
| Oil burner appliance | B139 Series / B140 series |
| Process appliances in refineries (NAICS 21111, 32411, 32511…) | Clause 16 exemptions may apply |
| Mobile food service (propane food truck) | Annex J |
| Hydrogen or H2/natural gas blends | Annex N |

## Key Takeaways
- B149.3 exists for **field approval**: it tells the AHJ and assembler what a safe, code-compliant field-assembled fuel system looks like.
- Scope starts at the appliance manual shut-off valve and covers everything fuel-related downstream.
- The AHJ, not the manufacturer, grants approval; documentation is the currency of that approval.
- Many refinery/petrochemical process appliances fall under Clause 16's NAICS-based exemptions rather than general field approval.
