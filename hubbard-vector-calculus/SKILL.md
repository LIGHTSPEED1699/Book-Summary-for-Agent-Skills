# Vector Calculus, Linear Algebra and Differential Forms: A Unified Approach (John Hubbard & Barbara Burke Hubbard, 5th ed., Matrix Editions)
**Edition**: 5th (2009). 837-page scan re-OCR'd with PP-OCRv6; 7 chapters + analysis appendix.

The unified thesis in one sentence: the derivative is a linear map (ch1), linear algebra is the technology of solving equations (ch2), quadratic forms classify second-order behavior (ch3), measure zero decides integration (ch4-5), and differential forms with d and Stokes unify every integral theorem — including Maxwell's equations (ch6).

Use this skill whenever the task is multivariable differentiation/optimization, manifold integration, change of variables, curvature, or turning vector-calculus identities into one structure.

## Chapter map
0. **Preliminaries** (ch00): reading math, quantifiers, sets, functions, reals, complex numbers.
1. **Vectors, Matrices, and Derivatives** (ch01): geometry of Rⁿ, limits, five big theorems, derivative as linear map, Jacobian, chain rule, MVT, C¹ criterion.
2. **Solving Equations** (ch02): row reduction, kernel/image/dimension formula, change of basis, eigenvalues, Perron-Frobenius, Newton's method, Kantorovich, superconvergence, inverse/implicit preview.
3. **Manifolds, Taylor Polynomials, Quadratic Forms, and Curvature** (ch03): manifolds and tangent spaces, Taylor polynomials, quadratic forms and signature, critical-point classification, Lagrange multipliers, spectral theorem, SVD and probability/PCA, curvature.
4. **Integration** (ch04): dyadic pavings, measure zero, Fubini, numerical methods and Monte Carlo, determinants as volume, change of variables, dominated convergence, Lebesgue.
5. **Integration over Manifolds** (ch05): k-parallelograms and Gram determinants, parametrizations, manifold volumes, curvature and integration, fractals and fractional dimension.
6. **Forms and Vector Calculus** (ch06): forms as integrands, orientation, exterior derivative, grad/curl/div unified, pullback, generalized Stokes, Maxwell's equations, potentials and Poincaré's lemma.
- **Appendix: Analysis**: real arithmetic and computer floats, Heine-Borel, chain rule, Newton superconvergence, inverse/implicit proofs, crossed partials, flat functions, Taylor with remainder, Stirling, Lebesgue measure.

## The one-page spine
- Df(a) is a linear map; the Jacobian is its matrix; chain rule = matrix multiplication.
- Newton + Kantorovich: check three numbers, get a guaranteed quadratically convergent root.
- Quadratic form signature classifies extrema, saddles, constrained optima, curvature.
- Measure zero is the integrability filter; |det DT| is the change-of-variables volume factor.
- k-volumes via Gram determinants; manifold density √det(DγᵀDγ).
- Forms + d + ∫_S dφ = ∫_∂S φ: one theorem replaces the whole vector-calculus zoo; d² = 0 is curl grad = div curl = no monopoles.
