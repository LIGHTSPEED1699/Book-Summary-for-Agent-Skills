# Glossary — Visual Complex Analysis (Needham)

Needham's vocabulary plus standard terms, as used across this skill.

- **Amplitwist**: the local effect of an analytic map at a point — common enlargement |f′| plus common rotation arg f′ of all infinitesimal vectors; the complex derivative itself.
- **Amplification / twist**: the magnification factor / rotation angle of an amplitwist.
- **Anticonformal**: angle-preserving with orientation reversed (e.g., conjugation, circle inversion); even compositions of reflections are conformal, odd ones anticonformal.
- **Argument Principle**: for analytic f on/inside a simple loop Γ: number of p-points inside = ν[f(Γ), p], counted with multiplicity.
- **Branch point**: point around which a multifunction's branches permute (√z at 0, log z at 0 and ∞); acts as an obstacle to Taylor convergence like a singularity.
- **Cauchy's Formula**: f(a) = (1/2πi)∮_L f(z)/(z−a) dz; general loops give ν[L,a]·f(a).
- **Cauchy's Theorem**: integral of an analytic function over a closed contour in a region of analyticity vanishes; two contours with the same endpoints and analytic between them give equal integrals.
- **Cross-ratio**: [z,a,b,c] = ((z−a)(b−c))/((z−b)(c−a)); the Möbius invariant; hyperbolic distance = log of a cross-ratio with the ideal endpoints.
- **Deformation Theorem**: the value of a contour integral is unchanged while the contour is dragged through points of analyticity; singularities are "pegs".
- **Duality (harmonic)**: the π/2-rotated flow Ĥ = iH of a sourceless irrotational field; swaps potential/stream function (Φ̂ = Ψ, Ψ̂ = −Φ); source ↔ vortex.
- **Flux F[X,K]**: ∫ (X·N) ds — normal flow across a curve; imaginary part of ∮ H dz via the Pólya field.
- **Green's function G_p**: steady temperature with unit source at p and zero boundary temperature; G_p = −ln|f| for a conformal map f with f(p) = 0; symmetric G_p(q) = G_q(p); conformally natural.
- **Harmonic function**: Φ with ΔΦ = 0; equals the real part of an analytic function (locally); satisfies the mean-value and maximum principles.
- **Index (of a singular point)**: net revolutions of the vector field V around a loop enclosing the point once; = winding number of V(Γ) about 0 = topological multiplicity as preimage of 0; root order n → n, pole order m → −m (Pólya field flips sign).
- **k-cells / k-work tubes**: the square grid formed by equipotentials and streamlines at spacing k; the physical construction grid for conformal maps.
- **Laurent series**: expansion with finitely (pole) or infinitely (essential) many negative powers near a singularity; residue = coefficient of (z−a)^{−1}.
- **Möbius transformation**: M(z) = (az+b)/(cz+d), ad−bc ≠ 0; generated by translations, dilative rotations, and complex inversion; classified elliptic/hyperbolic/loxodromic/parabolic by multiplier λ₁/λ₂.
- **Multifunction**: multi-valued z ↦ w (roots, log); branches permute around branch points; made single-valued on a Riemann surface.
- **Poincaré disc model**: hyperbolic plane as unit disc; lines = circles ⊥ boundary; motions = disc automorphisms (az+b)/(b̄z+ā).
- **Pólya vector field**: the field drawing H̄(z) at z; ∮ H dz = W[H̄] + i F[H̄] (work + i·flux); analytic ⟺ div = curl = 0.
- **Residue Res[f,a]**: coefficient of (z−a)^{−1} in the Laurent expansion; ∮ f dz = 2πi Σ ν[L,aₙ] Res[f,aₙ]; physically: circulation + i·source strength of the Pólya field.
- **Riemann mapping theorem**: any simply connected region (≠ ℂ) admits a 3-parameter family of conformal maps onto the unit disc; physically constructed by matching flow grids (dipole position + direction = the parameters).
- **Riemann sphere**: the plane plus ∞ via stereographic projection; natural domain of analytic functions; Möbius = its conformal automorphisms.
- **Stream function Ψ / potential Φ**: level curves of Ψ are streamlines; of Φ equipotentials; Ω = Φ + iΨ is the complex potential.
- **Winding number ν(L, p)**: net counterclockwise revolutions of (z−p) as z traverses loop L; locally constant in p; crossing rule computes it.
- **Work W[X,K] / circulation**: ∫ (X·T) ds — tangential integral; real part of ∮ H dz via the Pólya field; conservative/irrotational ⟺ vanishes on all loops.
