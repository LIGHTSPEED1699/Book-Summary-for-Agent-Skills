# Ch6–8 — Gestalt and Universals · Cybernetics and Psychopathology · Information, Language, and Society

## Ch6 — Gestalt and Universals (pattern as group invariance)
- Recognizing a form under translation/rotation/scaling = **invariance under a transformation group**. Wiener's measure: average Q(TS) over the group using the invariant group integral (Haar measure); sets transformable into each other have identical Q — a quantitative definition of "same form."
- Approximate invariance: integrate over part of the group when the integrand is small on the omitted region.
- **McCulloch's tables** as the first sketch of learned invariance detectors; the **group-scanning assembly** as brain hardware: layered retina-like structure, horizontal leads shifting thresholds so each layer fires when its lead comes on, cyclical traversal of layers — a permanent subassembly playing the role of an adder/multiplier for form.
- The scan's intrinsic period ≈ 0.1 s for direct shape comparison → identified with the **alpha rhythm** (~10 Hz EEG): a sweep rhythm like a TV carrier — strongest eyes-closed, disappears in deep sleep, overlaid when actually looking.
- **Sensory prosthesis**: the blind can store tactile/auditory impressions in visual form (association areas are transmodal — "not locks with a single key"). Replacing sight by sound needs an **artificial visual cortex**, not just receptors: make things that look alike sound alike. Capacity bound ≈ cortical area ratio sight:sound ≈ **100:1** → ~1% of normal vision bandwidth; but acuity scales differently (reading needs far less than scene vision), so it's worth building (McCulloch's three-photocell chord device: letter F as a chord).

## Ch7 — Cybernetics and Psychopathology (brain as machine-run gone traffic-jammed)
- Error tolerance first: the brain likely uses the Ch5 redundancy — multiple channels, **internuncial pools**, majority reporting ("What I tell you three times is true").
- Functional vs organic mental disease = **software vs hardware** failure: nothing in the cadaver's brain identifies a schizophrenic; the disorder lives in the circulating information and long-time synaptic permeabilities — "diseases of memory, of the circulating information kept by the brain in the active state."
- **Malignant worry = an expanding reverberating circuit**: circular processes either die out (specious present) or recruit more and more neurons until they occupy an inordinate part of the neuron pool; normal thought then lacks "room"; the pathology cascades from circulating into permanent memory. Machine analogue: an unrecoverable circular process; remedies = clear the machine, jolt it, disconnect a part.
- The brain has **no non-pathological clear** except sleep ("sleep over it"); deeper interventions: shock treatments (damage circulating memory — preferable to lobotomy when recent) and **prefrontal lobotomy** (destroys the capacity for maintained worry — "the conscience"; Wiener's ethical jab: "killing them makes custodial care still easier"). Psychoanalysis fits the model: stored information on many levels of accessibility, affectively toned; therapy rewrites the affect, not the record. Combined shock + psychotherapy = reverberation interrupt + deep-memory work.
- **Traffic theory of mind**: n-stage switching with per-stage success q gives total p = q^n (75% over 5 stages → 95%/stage; 90% → 98%/stage). Near capacity, multi-stage systems show **no warning then catastrophic jam**. Man runs the longest neuronal chains → performs near the overload edge; breakdown hits the **longest chains first = the "highest" faculties** (evidence: fever facilitates higher processes more — facilitation compounds along chain length).
- Brain geometry: gray ∝ A³, connectors ∝ A² → bigger brain = denser fiber traffic; convolution helps short-distance, hurts long-distance trunk lines → multi-center "higher" processes are least stable. Hemispheric dominance: one hemisphere carries the higher functions (Pasteur's right-hemisphere lesion → still did his best work; mirror lesion would have been crippling); forced left-to-right retraining → stuttering (writing retrained into the secondary hemisphere, speech left in the dominant one). Human brain possibly **past peak size** — "as far along the road to destructive specialization as the great nose horns of the titanotheres."

## Ch8 — Information, Language, and Society (the state as a communication system)
- Community = effective transmission of information: "the community extends only so far as there extends an effectual transmission." Measure group autonomy: decisions entering from outside vs decisions made inside.
- Ants: smell ≈ communal hormones, chitin-bound behavior; pre-linguistic intelligent communication exists via **attention signals** (the savage in the woods: meaning from shared moments of notice).
- Communal information ≠ sum of individual information; libraries clog by their own volume; cross-field relevance needs "Leibnizian catholicity" (Vannevar Bush's mechanical search helps only if someone already filed the book under the unfamiliar heading).
- **The market is not homeostatic**: it is a game in the von Neumann–Morgenstern sense — with ≥3 players, extreme indeterminacy, coalitions ending "in a welter of betrayals." The comforting "free competition = thermostat" doctrine lacks evidence.
- Knaves and fools: the fool's behavior is predictable as a rat in a maze — advertising, straw votes, opinion sampling as the science of exploiting gullibility. Small communities are homeostatic (public opinion disciplines the overreacher); large communities are not: the **"triple constriction" of the means of communication** (unprofitable media eliminated; owned by the wealthy; attract the power-hungry) puts the organism's sense organs in the hands of the anti-homeostatic class → **"the State is stupider than most of its components"** (like the wolf pack).
- Limits of social science (the epistemological core): precise science needs **loose coupling of observer and observed** (astronomy: we're too small to matter; atomic physics: too big to care). Social science has tight coupling both ways: the anthropologist's presence changes the community ("traduttore traditore"); investigating the stock market upsets the market; only short statistical runs available → "never good to more than a very few decimal places." Don't build exaggerated expectations; much must stay with the narrative historian.
- **Note — the chess machine** (first published minimax sketch): play out all moves n deep, assign conventional valuations (mate highest, being mated lowest, piece values near good-player values), back up by max–min, choose the max at depth n. "Might be as good a player as the vast majority of the human race." (1948 — before Shannon 1950.)

## Practitioner takeaways
- Diagnose any organization by its sense organs and feedback latency: who monitors what back, and whose interests capture the monitoring channel.
- Watch the p^(1/n) cliff in multi-stage pipelines (approval chains, microservice call graphs, org sign-offs): per-stage reliability must compound; failure appears suddenly, not gradually.
- Treat "the market self-corrects" and "bigger org = smarter org" as unproven homeostasis claims; demand the actual feedback loop.
