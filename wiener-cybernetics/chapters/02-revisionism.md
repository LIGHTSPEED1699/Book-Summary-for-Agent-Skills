# Ch2 — Revisionism: The Behaviorist and the Engineer

## The revision: from energy to information
- 19th-century view of automata (living and artificial): **power engineering** — the organism as heat engine, bookkeeping in energy and potential (Rashevsky school).
- The vacuum tube changed the paradigm: a device where a tiny input signal controls an almost unlimited external energy source, working at a low energy level. Neurons work exactly this way (power supplied by circulation, not by the signal).
- Therefore: "the newer study of automata, whether in the metal or in the flesh, is a branch of **communication engineering**" — cardinal notions: message, noise, quantity of information, coding.
- The automaton is coupled to the world by a flow of impressions and actions: receptors (photoelectric cells, radar, microphones ≈ sense organs), effectors (motors, solenoids, heating coils ≈ muscles), a central recombining apparatus, storage (memory), and rule-change from past data (learning).

## Purpose without animism: the feedback definition
- **Purpose = a behavior regulated by feedback against a criterion of failure.** The classic engineering statement: the effector's performance is monitored back and combined with the goal so that action is "regulated by some measure of the amount by which it has not yet been accomplished."
- No consciousness needed: the thermostat, the governor, the signalman's telltale, the navy's repeating of orders all exhibit goal-directed behavior in the only operationally meaningful sense. This rehabilitates teleology as feedback — "revisionism" against both vitalism and crude mechanism.

## Attack on strict behaviorism (Watson)
- Behaviorism's schema S→R with introspection banished fails on **circular systems**: the telephone answering device "Mary" — the outgoing message (answer) is itself part of the stimulus pattern; you cannot specify the act without reference to what the system does later.
- Watson's program (describe behavior only in stimulus-response terms, no mental terms) is self-undermining: the S-R chain is not linear but a feedback loop; the "stimulus" during an act includes the effectors' own results (proprioception).
- Likewise the introspectionists: the content of consciousness is not the right primitive either. The engineer's category — **the feedback loop, the message, the servo** — cuts across both schools and is the one that admits quantitative treatment.
- Physiological evidence, not metaphor: ataxia cases (tabes dorsalis = lost proprioceptive feedback; cerebellar tremor = broken proportioning of response to feedback) show purposeful movement **is** feedback control; remove the feedback, remove the purpose, keep the muscle.

## How to use this chapter
- When analyzing any goal-directed system (agent, controller, organization, animal): ask (1) what is the criterion signal, (2) what is monitored back, (3) where is the loop closed, (4) what happens when the feedback is delayed, attenuated, or lost (oscillation, ataxia, tremor). Behavior that "misses and overshoots" is a feedback signature, not a motivation mystery.
