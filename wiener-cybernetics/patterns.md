# Cross-chapter patterns (Cybernetics — Wiener)

1. **The same loop everywhere.** Thermostat, governor, signalman, voluntary motion, homeostasis, market, organization: draw sensor→comparator→effector→monitor and the analysis transfers. When a "behavior" explanation is invoked, demand the loop.
2. **Count the lags.** Stability is topology of A(iy) vs the point −1/λ; one or two lags tolerate unlimited feedback, three cascaded lags (kz³, ship steering) defeat any single loop — the fix is always an inner loop (rudder-position feedback), never more gain in the outer one.
3. **Causality is a design constraint, not a philosophy.** All physical operators integrate over the past; anything that appears to use the future is an undamped oscillator, i.e., the system talking to itself.
4. **Prediction has a spectrum-shaped floor.** The best predictor is matched to the ensemble of all similar histories, not the single track; irreducible noise sets the error. (Ch3 filter → Ch9 predictor coefficients → Nelson's blockade: exploit the statistically known opponent.)
5. **Two-level control: fast action, slow self-analysis.** Predictor: linear filter on the immediate past + non-linear fitting of coefficients from the long past. Learning machine: play with fixed valuations + periodically refit valuations from all recorded games. Adaptive control, STR, and agent self-improvement all inherit this split.
6. **Redundancy beats inspection.** Don't check every step; replicate mechanisms and take the majority report, with hot-spare searching like a telephone exchange. Applies from neurons to CI pipelines.
7. **Never-cleared memory changes the dynamics.** A machine is cleared between runs; the brain/agent/organization is one continuous run. Reverberations accumulate, recruit, and jam: malignant worry, habit, institutional pathologies. Interrupting the circuit (sleep, shock, retro, restructuring) is the only "clear."
8. **The compounding cliff p^(1/n).** Multi-stage systems (switching, approval chains, neuron chains, microservices) degrade invisibly then catastrophically; the longest chains — the highest functions — fail first.
9. **Observer coupling bounds the science.** Astronomy (too big), atomic physics (too small) permit loose coupling; social systems don't: measuring changes it, runs are short, accuracy is a few decimal places. Any social-sciences claim should be discounted by this before debating it.
10. **Who owns the sense organs decides the organism's intelligence.** Media/schools/church = the community's receptors and effectors; the triple constriction (profit, ownership, ambition) explains why large communities are stupider than their members. In any system with metrics, ask whose interests captured the monitoring channel.
11. **Literal-mindedness of built agencies.** The fables (Sorcerer's Apprentice, genie, Monkey's Paw) are Wiener's alignment ethics: the machine grants the programmed valuation, learned from the programmed experience. Audit valuations and training experience against what you actually want; "turning it off" is not a control you can assume.
12. **Self-organization = coupled oscillators + non-linear coupling.** Alpha rhythm clumps with adjacent spectral gaps, fireflies, circadian entrainment, AC busbars' virtual governor: look for frequency attraction as the mechanism wherever order without a conductor appears.
13. **Dichotomy is the cheap representation.** Binary recording minimizes cost of accuracy; Boolean logic minimizes the machinery of contingency; all-or-none neurons implement it. Continuous precision is what you pay extra for at the edges only.
14. **Information = negative entropy; measurement is physical.** Maxwell's demon closes the loop between thermodynamics and communication; any feedback controller's information must be budgeted in the entropy account (modern: transfer entropy, Landauer).
