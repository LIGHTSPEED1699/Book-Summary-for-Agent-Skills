# Cheatsheet — The Road to Reality (Penrose)

## Foundations
- Three worlds: physical / mental / mathematical; effectiveness of math = the framing mystery.
- Complex numbers: i² = −1; e^{iθ} = cosθ + i sinθ; z^n roots n-fold; log z multivalued (+2πi n).
- Holomorphic ⟹ analytic; Cauchy ∮f dz = 0 (simply connected); Riemann surfaces make multifunctions single-valued.
- Hyperfunctions: generalized functions as boundary values of holomorphic functions; δ = jump across real axis.

## Classical geometry
- Gaussian curvature K = κ₁κ₂; intrinsic (Theorema Egregium); Gauss-Bonnet ∫K dA + ∮k_g ds = 2πχ.
- Quaternions: i²=j²=k²=ijk=−1; unit quaternions = SU(2) → SO(3) 2:1; belt trick (2π = −1, 4π = 1).
- Octonions: non-associative; exceptional groups G₂ etc.
- Manifold: charts/atlases; tangent/cotangent; vectors as derivations.
- Forms: wedge ∧ antisymmetric; exterior derivative d, d² = 0; ⋆ Hodge star; Laplacian Δ = dδ + δd.
- Fundamental theorem of exterior calculus: ∫_M dα = ∫_{∂M} α (Newton/Green/Stokes/Gauss unified).
- de Rham H^q = closed/exact; Betti numbers b_q; Euler χ = Σ(−1)^q b_q.
- Lie groups/algebras: [T_a,T_b] = f_{ab}^c T_c; classical families SO, SU, Sp; SU(2)→SO(3).

## Relativity
- Minkowski metric signature (−,+,+,+); interval ds² = −c²dt² + dx²; proper time dτ.
- Rapidity: v = c tanh φ; boosts add in φ. E² = p²c² + m²c⁴; E = mc² at rest.
- Curved spacetime: connection Γ, geodesics, Riemann R^a_{bcd}; Ricci R_ab, scalar R; Weyl C_abcd (trace-free).
- Einstein equation: G_ab = R_ab − ½R g_ab = 8πG T_ab / c⁴.
- Noether: symmetry (Killing vector) ⟹ conserved current.
- Maxwell as gauge: F = dA (2-form), dF = 0, d⋆F = J; potentials = connections, F = curvature.

## Quantum
- Amplitudes ∈ ℂ; probability = |amplitude|²; superposition; U (unitary) vs R (reduction).
- Spin-½: 2-component spinor; Stern-Gerlach; Riemann sphere of spin states; 2π rotation → −1.
- Commutators [x,p] = iℏ; uncertainty ΔxΔp ≥ ℏ/2.
- Entanglement: non-factorizable states; Bell inequalities violated (no local hidden variables); no-cloning; teleportation = entanglement + 2 classical bits.
- Dirac: (iγ^a ∂_a − m)ψ = 0; predicts antiparticles; spin-½ forced by linearity + SR.
- Standard model gauge: SU(3)_color × SU(2)_L × U(1)_Y; Higgs breaks to U(1)_em.
- QFT: fields quantized; Fock space; Feynman diagrams = perturbation bookkeeping; renormalization absorbs infinities into parameters.
- Graviton: spin-2 (helicity ±2), from quantizing metric perturbations; non-renormalizable.

## Cosmology & entropy
- Friedmann models K = 0,±1; expansion, redshift, CMB.
- Black-hole entropy S = A/4Gℏ; Hawking T = ℏκ/2π; four laws of BH mechanics ↔ thermodynamics.
- Second law + past hypothesis; Weyl curvature hypothesis: low entropy = Weyl ≈ 0 at big bang.
- Inflation: e-folds ~60; Penrose critique — assumes special inflaton, doesn't explain low Weyl.
- Gravitational OR: τ ≈ ℏ/E_G (E_G = gravitational self-energy of superposed-mass difference).

## Twistors (Part V)
- Twistor T = (ω^A, π_{A'}) ∈ ℂ⁴; incidence ω^A = i μ^A + x^{AA'} π_{A'}.
- Spacetime point ↔ line ℂP¹ in projective twistor space PT; light ray ↔ point of PT.
- Penrose transform: H¹(PT, O(2s−2)) ⟺ massless helicity-s field on spacetime.
- Conformal structure primary; points derived.
