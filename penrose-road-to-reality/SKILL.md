# The Road to Reality (Penrose, 2004)

Use when working at the physics-math interface: Penrose's 35-chapter route from complex numbers to twistor theory, organized here into the book's five parts. The through-line: physical law is geometric (connections, curvature, cohomology), quantum mechanics' measurement structure is the unsolved crack, and the next revolution must be mathematically compelled AND experimentally exposed.

## Core theses (apply as defaults)
1. Geometry is physics: curvature = field strength (gauge), connection = potential, Noether = conservation; Einstein's equation is geometry sourced by stress-energy.
2. The complex numbers and holomorphicity are not conveniences — quantum amplitudes, spinors, and twistor theory all live in ℂ (and ℍ, 𝕆).
3. Quantum mechanics' U/R split is a real physical problem; Penrose's gravitational objective reduction (τ ≈ ℏ/E_G) is the proposed fix.
4. The arrow of time is an initial-condition law: Weyl curvature = 0 at the big bang; gravitational entropy = horizon area (S = A/4).
5. Cohomology and bundles are the language: charges, monopoles, anomalies, and (via the Penrose transform) fields themselves.
6. Demand mathematical beauty AND experimental refutation — the great-theory audit's two columns; unfalsifiable beauty (string landscape) fails.
