# Chapter 12 — Tables (pp. 393–514) and Diagrams (pp. 515–523)

## Overview
- The Tables section is the numeric backbone of CSA C22.1:24: ampacities, correction factors, conduit fill, overcurrent/bonding/grounding sizes, clearances, hazardous-area extents. TOC at pp. 393–396. Tables are normative — cite exact values, never estimate.
- Several 2024-ed. tables are **deleted** (6, 9, 10A–C, 11, 16A/B, 20, 38, 39, 42) and several are **re-pointed** (26 → Table D16; 46/47/49/54/55 → Diagrams 1/2/3/4/5). Pre-2024 cross-references may not resolve.
- Six Diagrams follow the Tables (pp. 515–523).

## Gotchas
- Correction chain for ampacity: base table (1–4) → 5A ambient → 5B (free air spaced) or 5C (raceway count) → 5D only for tray with 12E. Do not mix 5B with raceway tables or 5C with free-air tables.
- Table 5A also corrects Table 60, not just 1–4.
- 53% fill applies to **one** conductor; 31% to two; 40% to three or more — pick the matching 9C–9H area table, and use Table 9A/9B diameters for the conduit itself.
- Box fill is Tables 22/23, not "Table 10" (deleted); conduit bending radii are Table 7 (and 15 for HV cable), not 6A–6K (those are conductor dimensions).
- Diagram 3's asterisk convention and Diagram 1/2 terminal letters are load-bearing details — quote them exactly.
