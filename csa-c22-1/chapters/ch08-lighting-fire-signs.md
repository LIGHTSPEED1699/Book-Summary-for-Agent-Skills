# Chapter 8 — Lighting, Fire Alarms & Signs (Sections 30, 32, 34)

## Overview
Section 30: lighting equipment — location/protection, live parts, wiring, lampholders, discharge lighting (≤1000 V and >1000 V), recessed luminaires, outdoor floodlighting, pole-line wiring, extra-low-voltage systems. Section 32: fire alarm systems, smoke/CO alarms, fire pumps. Section 34: signs and outline lighting — neon supplies, high-voltage wiring.

## Section 30 — Installation of lighting equipment

### Circuits, location, live parts (30-100s, 30-300s)
- **30-102/30-104**: dwelling lighting circuits 15 A; other lighting ≤20 A at ≤347 V; 40 A exceptions (mogul-base HID, tungsten halogen, integral-OCP luminaires).
- **30-310**: luminaire-as-raceway conductors ≥600 V, 90 °C (NM cable OK if 90 °C); **30-312**: air space from combustible shades; **30-314**: guard rigid luminaires <2.1 m above floor; **30-316**: guard gym lamps; **30-324**: outdoor arc lamps ≥2.5 m above ground.
- **30-318**: damp/wet locations need marked luminaires; **30-320**: damp or within 1.5 m horizontal / 2.5 m vertical of grounded metal — wall-switch control; switches ≥1 m from tub/shower, or ≥500 mm with Class A GFCI.

### Wiring and lampholders (30-400s, 30-600s)
- **30-404**: min No. 18 AWG luminaire conductors; **30-408**: ceiling-box connections 90 °C insulation, ampacity capped at 60 °C values; **30-410**: show-window luminaires ≥125 °C conductors in junction boxes; **30-412**: No. 14 AWG taps on 20 A circuits ≤7.5 m.
- **30-600**: identified conductor to screwshell; **30-606**: weatherproof lampholders in wet/damp; **30-608**: pendant leads No. 14 AWG (mogul/medium), No. 18 AWG (intermediate/candelabra), twisted if >900 mm.

### Discharge lighting ≤1000 V (30-700s) and >1000 V (30-800s)
- **30-702**: no oil-filled transformers; **30-706**: >300 V open-circuit equipment prohibited in dwellings unless live parts not exposed during relamping; **30-710**: switches rated ≥2× lamp/transformer current, 15/20 A ≤347 V fluorescent breakers marked "SWD"; **30-712**: branch load from ballast amperes, not lamp watts.
- **30-802**: >1000 V prohibited in dwelling units; **30-806**: secondary OCV ≤15 000 V (≤7500 V open core-and-coil), secondary current ≤240 mA (≤120 mA if OCV >7500 V); **30-810**: no series/parallel HV windings except midpoint-grounded pairs; **30-814**: luminous-tube-sign cable — ≤6 m in metal raceway, ≤16 m non-metallic; **30-822**: mark "CAUTION" in ≥25 mm letters.

### Recessed luminaires (30-900s)
- **30-902**: Type Non-IC — 13 mm from combustibles, 76 mm from thermal insulation; **30-904**: "Non-IC, marked spacings" per manufacturer; **30-906**: Type IC may contact insulation/combustibles; **30-908**: non-combustible-surface units only on non-combustibles.
- **30-910**: junction box ≥30 cm from luminaire, within 35 cm of access opening; access opening ≥180 cm² (min 15 cm) or alternate 400 cm² (≥20 cm); integral supply boxes not pass-through unless marked.

### Outdoor floodlighting (30-1000s)
- **30-1004**: underground wiring — rigid conduit, MI/Al/Cu-sheathed cable, or direct burial (Rule 12-012); **30-1006**: disconnect per pole + anti-climb sign unless conductors guarded (conduit, ≥1 m from ladder, or barriers); **30-1010**: pole-top branch O/C ≤100 A.
- **30-1016**: transformers guarded on poles, ≥5 m above accessible locations, or fenced per Rules 26-300 to 26-324; **30-1020**: secondary switches ≥125% of load; **30-1022**: solidly grounded supply (>300 V circuits may be ungrounded/impedance-grounded).

### Exposed pole-line wiring (30-1100s) and ELV (30-1200s)
- **30-1102**: stranded Cu ≥No. 12 AWG; **30-1106**: ≥4 m above grade (≥5 m over driveways); **30-1108**: ≥1 m from combustibles; **30-1116**: messenger cable required for spans >12 m (permanent leads) or puncture-type lampholders; **30-1118**: branch O/C ≤30 A.
- **30-1202**: ELV supply ≤150 V-to-ground via isolating transformer secondary; ELV side not grounded; **30-1206**: cable lighting — dry locations only, never in bathrooms, bare conductors ≥2.2 m above floor, not through walls/ceilings.

## Section 32 — Fire alarm systems, smoke/CO alarms, fire pumps

### Fire alarm systems (32-100s)
- **32-100**: copper conductors, ≥300 V insulation; No. 16 AWG pulled in raceways, No. 19 AWG laid in raceways or integral 2–3-conductor assemblies, No. 22 AWG integral 4+; >7 strands bunch-tinned or compression-terminated; optical fibre for data links per CAN/ULC-S524; **32-102**: totally enclosed metal raceway, armoured cable, rigid non-metallic conduit, or ENT in ≥50 mm masonry/concrete; combustible-construction buildings may use NMD or fire alarm cable; wiring independent of all other wiring.
- **32-104**: bond per Section 10; **32-106**: dual terminals independently terminated; **32-108**: separate supply circuit, disconnect coloured red, lockable ON.

### Smoke and CO alarms (32-200)
- **32-200**: dwelling alarms on a lighting or lighting+receptacle circuit; **not** on GFCI/AFCI-protected circuits unless the alarm has integral battery backup; no disconnecting means between alarm and its overcurrent device (the cross-connection Rule 26-656 references).

### Fire pumps (32-300s)
- **32-300**: conductors ≥125% of fire pump motor FLC (or 125% of the sum: fire pump + jockey pump + auxiliaries), protected against fire exposure per NBC; **32-304**: separate service box permitted, may be remote; **32-306**: only a labelled fire-pump breaker (lockable closed, sized to carry locked-rotor current indefinitely) between supply and controller.
- **32-308**: transfer switch dedicated to fire pump, marked for fire pump service; **32-310**: no overload protection on the branch; **32-312**: ground-fault protection prohibited.

## Section 34 — Signs and outline lighting

### General (34-100s)
- **34-100**: disconnect opens all ungrounded conductors; integral or within sight and ≤9 m, or lockable open; **34-102**: switches/flashers rated ≥2× neon supply/transformer/ballast current; **34-104**: thermally protected fluorescent ballasts (except simple reactance); **34-106**: >2.2 m above grade or mechanically protected, clear of overhead conductors.
- **34-110**: bond per Section 10 (exemptions: parts ≤50 mm and ≥19 mm from tubing, wire-ties, Class 2 subassemblies ≤30 V); **34-112**: non-combustible bushings on sign leads.

### Neon supplies (34-300s)
- **34-300**: secondary OCV ≤15 000 V and ≤7500 V-to-ground; **34-302**: secondary ground-fault protection except ≤6000 V isolated secondaries or integral porcelain/glass housing.
- **34-304**: open-type (core-and-coil) supplies only in dry locations; **34-306**: damp/wet-rated supplies where required; **34-308**: accessible — attic access ≥900 × 600 mm, passageway ≥900 × 600 mm, walkway ≥300 mm; **34-310**: overcurrent ≤30 A per supply; **34-312**: outputs never paralleled or series-connected.

### High-voltage wiring (34-400s)
- **34-400**: HV cable in enclosures or raceways (flexible metal ≥16 trade size); bends per Table 15; 38 mm separation from conducting/combustibles (≤100 Hz) or 44 mm (>100 Hz); exposed runs in GTO sleeving ≤300 mm; field wiring ≥No. 18 AWG; one HV cable per conduit.
- **34-402/34-404**: show-window HV cables in GTO sleeving, no mechanical duty; supply cable ≤6 m in metal raceway, ≤16 m in non-metallic; **34-406**: connections inaccessible — electrode receptacle or enclosed, insulation extending ≥100 mm (damp/wet) or 65 mm (dry) beyond raceway; **34-408**: flexible metal conduit may serve as bonding conductor; external No. 12 AWG bonding on non-metallic conduit at 38/44 mm.

## Key Takeaways
- Lighting circuit limits are conservative: 15 A dwelling, 20 A general, 30 A pole-line, 40 A special luminaires, 100 A pole-top floodlighting.
- Recessed luminaire spacing keys off the marking (Non-IC / marked spacings / IC); junction-box location/access are dimension-driven (30 cm / 35 cm / 180 cm²).
- Fire alarm circuits: dedicated, supervised, independent wiring; smoke/CO alarms cannot share GFCI/AFCI protection without battery backup. Fire pumps invert normal protection: 125% FLC conductors, no overload protection, no ground-fault protection.
- Neon/sign HV work hinges on the ≤7500 V-to-ground ceiling, GTO sleeving, and frequency-based 38/44 mm clearances.
