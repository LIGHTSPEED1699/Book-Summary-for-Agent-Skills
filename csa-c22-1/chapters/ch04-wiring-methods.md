# Chapter 4 — Wiring Methods (Section 12)

## Overview
Section 12 (pp. 108–156): conductor/cable installation, NMD90/NMWU + armoured/MI cable, raceways and conduits (fill, bends, supports, bonding), busways/wireways/cable trays, and box/pull-box sizing.

## Core Content

### General (12-000s–12-100s)
- **12-000** applies to LV/ELV wiring; excludes Class 2 (Sec 16), Sec 54/56/60, factory-integrated conductors.
- **12-010** plenums: only marked equipment; plenum cable per 2-130/12-100.
- **12-012** underground: cover per Table 53; −150 mm with protection ≥50 mm each side; rise protection → ≥2 m above grade; marking tape halfway to grade.
- **12-100/12-102** suitability; types per Table 19; cords per Table 11A; T90 Nylon + oil → 60 °C unless PR/GR marked.
- **12-106** all circuit conductors in one multiconductor cable (parallel exception per 12-108); single >200 A not encircled by magnetic material.
- **12-108** parallel: ≥1/0 AWG; same size/insulation/termination/material/length; no splices; separate grounded conductor per set.
- **12-112** splices: equivalent insulation, accessible; underground repairs in boxes ≥1 m above grade or direct-burial kits.
- **12-116** terminations: fine-strand → fine-strand connectors or ferrules; **no ferrules on solid conductors**.
- **12-118** aluminum: joint compound unless marked; equipment marked for Al; wet-location terminations corrosion-protected.
- **12-120** vertical support per Table 21: No. 14-8 → 30 m; >750 kcmil → 10 m Cu / 25 m Al.

### Open and exterior wiring (12-200s/12-300s)
- **12-202** open conductors generally in raceways; **12-204** dead-ends ≤300 mm from last insulator; **12-206** junctions in boxes.
- **12-304** clearances: 4 m residential driveways, 5 m commercial/industrial, 3.5 m pedestrian; ≥1 m from windows/doors. **12-306** supports ≤3 m. **12-308** spans ≤4.5 m (NS cable ≤40 m; sag Table 72). **12-310** ≥2.5 m walkable roof / ≥1 m peaked.

### NMD90 / NMWU (12-550–12-576)
- **12-552** max 300 V; **12-554** combustible construction + others where acceptable.
- **12-556** continuous lengths, joints in boxes; airspace 25 mm ducts / 50 mm chimneys / 150 mm cleanouts; never staple on edge.
- **12-560** support within 300 mm of boxes, ≤1.5 m; holes in joists/studs = support; fished cable exempt.
- **12-562** not embedded in plaster; **12-564/566** ≥32 mm from member edges or protector plate/bushing; **12-576** bonding per Section 10.

### Armoured cable (12-600s); MI/sheathed (12-700s)
- **12-602** any construction; direct-burial type for underground/masonry/weather; **12-608** armour continuity; **12-610** insulating bushings (mandatory ≥No. 8); **12-614** bends ≥6× OD; **12-616** concealed ≥32 mm or protector plate.
- MI/Al/Cu-sheathed: 12-700 use, 12-706 supporting, 12-712 bends, 12-714 terminations, 12-718 sheath temperature.

### Raceway rules (12-900–12-944)
- **12-902** conductors per Table 19; **12-904** all circuit conductors in same metal raceway; mixed sources need barriers (1.34 mm steel / 1.5 mm non-metallic).
- **12-906** bushings, insulated-type for ≥No. 8; **12-910** fill: ≤200 conductors; Table 8 fill %: 1→53%, 2→31%, ≥3→40%; conductor dims Tables 6A–6K; conduit areas Tables 9A–9H.
- **12-912** no splices in raceways (except busways/wireways/trays/removable-cover surface raceway); **12-914** ≥No. 8 stranded.
- **12-916/918** electrical + mechanical continuity; **12-924** bend radius per Table 7 (16 → 102 mm; 155 → 762 mm).
- **12-928** seal underground entries; **12-934** damage protection <2 m; **12-942** max four 90° bends between draw-in points.

### Metal conduit (12-1000s)
- **12-1004** min 16 trade size (12 TS FMC ≤1.5 m exception); **12-1006** tapered threads, no running threads; **12-1008** ≥3 threads engaged.
- **12-1010** supports: 1.5 m (16/21), 2 m (27/35), 3 m (41+); mixed sizes → smallest spacing; **FMC ≤1.5 m + within 300 mm of every box/fitting**.

### Non-metallic conduits
- **PVC (12-1100s):** not in thermal insulation; ≤75 °C; supports 750 mm (16–27) → 2.5 m (155) (12-1114); expansion joint >45 mm growth; **separate bonding conductor required** (12-1122; meter-loop exception).
- **LFMC (12-1300s):** supports ≤1.5 m + 300 mm of boxes (12-1308).
- **EMT (12-1400s):** wet/outdoor/concrete OK; never direct burial; within 1 m of boxes + 12-1010 spacing (12-1406); min 16 trade; separate insulated bonding conductor in concrete/wet/outdoor (12-1414).
- **ENT (12-1500s):** mechanical protection; separate bonding conductor (12-1514). **Surface raceway (12-1600s):** exposed dry only; ≤50 °C; no splices except removable covers.

### Busways (12-2000s), wireways (12-2100s), cable tray (12-2200s)
- **12-2000** busways exposed only; not wet/damp unless marked; support ≤1.5 m; splitters where >No. 6 Cu / No. 4 Al connections.
- **12-2012** downsize without OCPD: ≤15 m and ≥⅓ upstream rating; **12-2014** branch-circuit busway length ≲ amp rating.
- **12-2100** wireways exposed/dry unless marked; **12-2106** taps insulated + accessible.
- **12-2200** tray clearances: 150 mm tier-to-tier (300 if ≥50 mm cables); 300 mm to ceilings/heating; 600 mm one side (≤1 m width) / both sides (>1 m).
- **12-2202** tray cables need metal sheath/armour; TC/TC-ER industrial (single ≥1/0); single R90/RW75/RW90 ≥1/0 in vaults/service rooms.
- **12-2208** metal tray bonding ≤15 m per 10-616; exempt if all cables interlocked-armour/metal-sheathed → post warning notice.

### Boxes, cabinets, pull boxes (12-3000s)
- **12-3030** mixed voltage sources: barrier ≥1.3 mm steel / 1.6 mm non-metallic (or exceptions).
- **12-3032** OCPD enclosures not junction boxes; exceptions: single feeder tap w/ independent clamps, additions ≤75% fill, pass-through ≤40% fill.
- **12-3034** fill counting: through-conductor = 1; non-leaving = 0; fixture wires exempt; deduct 1 per stud/hickey, 1 per pair of capped connectors, 2 per flush device; deep devices 32 cm³/cm.
- **Table 22** (p. 477): 14→24.6 mL, 12→28.7, 10→36.9, 8→45.1, 6→73.7. **Table 23** (p. 478): 4×1½" octagon → 10×#14; 3×2×2½" → 8×#14.
- **12-3036** pull boxes ≥No. 4: wall-to-cover ≥ trade diameter of largest raceway.

## Key Takeaways
- Fill: Table 8 (53/31/40%) + Tables 6A-6K/9A-9H; ≤200 conductors; ≤4×90° bends per run.
- Supports: metal conduit 1.5/2/3 m by trade size; FMC/LFMC/NMD90 1.5 m + 300 mm; PVC 750 mm–2.5 m; EMT within 1 m of boxes.
- Non-metallic raceways (PVC/ENT/HDPE) need a separate bonding conductor.
- Box fill: count → deduct devices/clamps → Table 23 (uniform) or Table 22 (mixed).
- 2024 renumbering: PVC = 12-1100s, RTRC = 12-1200s, busways = 12-2000s, boxes = 12-3000s.
