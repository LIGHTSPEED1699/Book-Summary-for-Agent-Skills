# Chapter 9 — High Voltage, Elevators, Cranes, Welders, Theatre, Emergency Power (Sections 36-46)

## Overview
Specialty Sections beyond ordinary premises wiring: HV stations >750 V (36), vertical transportation (38), cranes and hoists (40), welders (42), theatres (44), NBC-mandated emergency power for life safety, unit equipment, exit signs (46). Sections 48 and 50 are deleted in C22.1:24.

## Core Content

### Section 36 — High-voltage installations (>750 V)
- Utility consult first (36-000 2)); authorized-person-only access (36-004); DANGER signs on cables/trays ≤10 m, fences ≤15 m, no switching under load, posted single-line diagrams (36-006); bare conductors only outdoors, vaults (26-350..26-356), trays, authorized rooms — else conduit/EMT/bus/sheathed-armoured/TC (36-100 2)); TC+LV tray barrier ≥1.34 mm (36-100 3)); service ≥ No. 6 Cu strength (36-100 6)); bends Table 15 (36-102); details 36-104..36-118.
- Protection in all ungrounded conductors — breaker or fuses behind a group-operated visible-break device (36-204); entrance-point service (36-200), ratings per 14-012 (36-202); enclosed indoor gear (36-206); instrument transformers per 26-260 (36-210); outdoor spacing per Table 35 (36-212); building entry: load-break device + visible isolators (36-214); station ground = ≥4 rods + No. 2/0 AWG bare Cu grid buried 150–600 mm looped around equipment, rebar acceptable (36-302); balance 36-300..36-310.

### Section 38 — Elevators, dumbwaiters, material lifts, escalators, moving walks, accessibility lifts
- Circuits ≤300 V; 750 V exceptions: door operators, drives, brakes, M-G sets, controllers, car HVAC (38-003); live parts enclosed (38-004); travelling cables min No. 14 lighting / No. 20 control / No. 26 elsewhere (38-012); ampacity per 28-106/Table 27 or nameplate (38-013); feeder demand Table 62 (38-014); dedicated circuits — car lights (38-022), machine-room lighting + 15 A duplex (38-023), pit light + 15 A receptacle (38-024); required lighting never downstream of a GFCI (38-023 2), 38-024 2)); elevator-only hoistway wiring (38-037); cables suspended both ends (38-041).
- One lockable, externally operated drive disconnect, visible on room entry, extra if >9 m (38-051); per-source disconnects with warnings (38-052); numbered car-light (38-053) and HVAC (38-054) disconnects; no automatic reclosing (38-051 10)); OCPD per 14/16, overload 28-302 (38-061); selective coordination (38-062); bonding to cars, frames, escalator metal (38-081..38-084); Class A GFCI for 125 V receptacles (pit/hoistway/car-top/wellway/machine-room; sump pumps exempt) (38-085); regenerative power absorbed, disconnect drops both sources (38-091).

### Section 40 — Electric cranes and hoists
- Supply per Table 58 (40-002); conduit/EMT/armoured/MI/sheathed or festoon cord with take-up (40-004); OCPD per 28-200 + 14-104 (40-006); disconnect (40-008).
- Contact conductors ≥ supply, min No. 4 Cu / No. 2 Al ≤18 m, No. 2 Cu / 1/0 Al beyond (40-010); spacing ≥150 mm horizontal, 75 monorail, 200 non-horizontal, ≥38 mm clearance (40-012); supports ≤6 m, rigid ≤4.5 m (40-014); track-as-phase ≤300 V via isolating transformer (40-018); guard unless ≥6 m (40-020); bond per 10-700 (40-024).

### Section 42 — Electric welders
- Receptacles may be rated below OCPD, never below rated primary current; welder-only labelling (42-004).
- Transformer/inverter: ampacity = rated primary × Table 42A, groups 100/85/70/60% (42-006); OCPD ≤200% (42-008); disconnect (42-010); motor-generator per Sections 4/28 × Table 42B (42-012); resistance: 70% seam, 50% manual, × Table 42C, groups largest + 60% (42-014); OCPD ≤300% (42-016); isolating switch ≥ ampacity (42-018); kVA at 50% duty on nameplate (42-020).

### Section 44 — Theatre installations
- Travelling shows need a permit before first performance (44-100); stage/wing/pit/booth wiring in metal raceway or sheathed/armoured cable (44-102).
- Switchboards: dead-front + hood (44-200); pilot lamp on independent ≤15 A circuit (44-206); OCPD each ungrounded conductor, ≤100 A for ≤120 V luminaires (44-210); dimmers dead when open (44-212); 125 °C insulation (44-216); portable: fused master kills all but pilot, Type S/SO/ST cord (44-250/44-252).
- Stage: footlights troughed (44-300); metal ≥0.78 mm, portable ≥0.53 mm (44-302); terminals ≥13 mm from metal (44-304); guarded lamps (44-306); OCPD per 30-104 (44-316); >100 W pendants guarded ≤13 mm mesh (44-318); border cord overhead only (44-320); arc loop ≥600 mm (44-322); ≥30 A gallery receptacle (44-324); scenery/festoon 44-350..44-356.

### Section 46 — Emergency power supply, unit equipment, exit signs, life safety systems
- NBC-required emergency supplies for life safety systems and exit signs; unit equipment to C22.2 No. 141 (46-000/46-002); capacity for all loads (46-100); posted instructions, monthly testing (46-102); batteries charged (46-104); no dark area from one lamp failure, no other loads on emergency circuits (46-106); independent wiring — raceway/armoured/sheathed/PVC/ENT ≥50 mm in masonry, NMD in combustible per 12-566..12-570 (46-108).
- Central supply: battery ≥91% voltage for NBC period (≥30 min) auto-charged, or CSA C282 auto-start generator (46-202); fire-protected generator wiring (46-204); auto transfer (46-206); trouble signals (46-210); remote lamps ≤5% drop (46-212); unit equipment: bottom ≥2 m (46-302), receptacles ≥2.5 m up / ≤1.5 m away, hardwired above 250 V or 24 A, auto-actuation (46-304); remote lamps ≤5% (46-306); exit signs: dedicated circuit, may share emergency lighting (46-400).

## Key Takeaways
- Section 36 is a parallel HV regime: utility first, guard/label all, four bare-conductor locations, engineered ground grid (≥4 rods + No. 2/0 Cu loop).
- One lockable numbered disconnect per function: drive (38-051), per-source (38-052), car lights (38-053), HVAC (38-054), welders (42-010, 42-018); no auto reclosing on elevators.
- Demand factors slash feeders: Table 62 elevators, 100/85/70/60% welder groups, largest + 60% resistance; welder OCPD oversized ≤200%/≤300%.
- Emergency wiring independent end-to-end (46-108); exit signs dedicated circuit; unit equipment hinges on mounting height, receptacle proximity, auto-actuation.
