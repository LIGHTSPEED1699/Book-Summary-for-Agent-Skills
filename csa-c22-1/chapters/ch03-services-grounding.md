# Chapter 3 — Consumer's Services, Grounding and Bonding (Sections 6 & 10)

## Overview
Section 6 (pp. 83–89) covers the supply service: number of services, service equipment, service conductors, metering. Section 10 (pp. 98–107) covers system grounding, grounding conductors/electrodes, bonding, and equipotential bonding.

## Core Content

### Number and arrangement of services
- **6-102** supply services permitted; **6-104** consumer's services per building; **6-106** multiple supply systems; **6-110** three-wire services.
- **6-112** support for attachment of overhead conductors; **6-114** service terminations; **6-116** service head location.

### Service equipment (6-200s)
- **6-200**: one service box per consumer's service; multiple boxes only via dual-lug meter mount ≤600 A / 150 V-to-ground, outdoors.
- **6-202**: multiple occupancy — separate service box per subdivision (or multi-service equipment); omitted if main OCPDs protect the subdivision.
- **6-204**: service-box fuseholders need their own spring-closed/catch-secured door.
- **6-206** location: per supply authority; readily accessible; close to service entry; NOT in closets/bathrooms/stairways, >30 °C rooms, hazardous locations, headroom <2 m, below flood elevation. Lockable disconnect OK (2)); outdoor/pole disconnect if weatherproof + ≥2 m or protected (3)).
- **6-208** conductor location; **6-210** oil switches; **6-212** wiring space; **6-214** marking.

### Service conductors (6-300s)
- **6-300** underground; **6-302** overhead; **6-304** MI/Al-sheathed cable; **6-306** service raceway min trade size 21; **6-308** service neutrals; **6-310** splices; **6-312** condensation.

### Metering — 6-402 to 6-412 (⚠️ the 6-400s, not 6-500s)
- **6-402** meter loops; **6-404** instrument transformer enclosures; **6-406** meter disconnect; **6-408** meter location; **6-410** meter space; **6-412** impedance-grounded metering.

### Grounding conductors and electrodes (10-100s)
- **10-102** electrodes; **10-104** electrode spacing/interconnection; **10-108** lightning down conductors.
- **10-110** no cutout/switch in grounding conductor unless it disconnects all energy sources.
- **10-112** Cu/Al/other; insulated or bare; corrosion-resistant.
- **10-114** ≥ No. 6 AWG Cu / No. 4 AWG Al; smaller OK if ≥ current-carrying conductors.
- **10-116** continuous; protected; magnetic sleeve bonded both ends; **insulated** in raceway with service conductors unless ≤15 m and ≤2 90° bends.
- **10-118** connection to electrodes.

### System grounding by configuration
- **Solidly grounded:** 10-200 DC systems; 10-206 AC systems required solidly grounded; 10-208 AC conductor to ground; 10-210 supply-authority services; 10-212 separately derived AC; 10-214 portable generators.
- **Impedance grounded (10-300s):** 10-300 qualified persons; 10-302 use + Table 17 alarm/de-energize conditions; 10-308 conductor ≥ No. 12 Cu / No. 10 Al, white, insulated, routed with circuit conductors.
- **Ungrounded (10-400s):** 10-400 ground-fault detection, no distributed grounded conductor, qualified persons; 10-402 equipment grounding.

### Bonding (10-500s → 10-616)
- General: **10-500** no current over bonding conductors; **10-504** dissimilar metals; **10-506** continuity.
- **10-600** fixed equipment bonding; **10-602** parallel runs; **10-604** service equipment continuity; **10-608** loosely jointed metal raceways → bonding conductor.
- **10-610** bonding means: FMC/LFMC armour NOT deemed bonding — run one inside; MI sheath non-Cu/Al not deemed; single-conductor cable isolated at one end needs separate bonding conductor.
- **10-612** splices/taps OK; bonding conductors in one box held in contact; insulated in raceway with circuit conductors unless ≤15 m / ≤2 quarter bends.
- **10-614** connections: lugs/pressure connectors/clamps; **solder-only prohibited**; bond at every outlet box; continuity survives cover removal/device replacement.
- **10-616** size per Table 16 (p. 463): jumper ← OCPD rating; service bonding ← ampacity of largest ungrounded conductor; parallel groups ← per-group size ÷ groups; never > current-carrying conductors; metal raceway or integral cable bonding deemed compliant.

### Table 16 quick sizing (p. 463)
| OCPD | Cu | Al |
|---|---|---|
| ≤20 A | 14 | 12 |
| 30 A | 12 | 10 |
| 60 A | 10 | 8 |
| 100 A | 8 | 6 |
| 200 A | 6 | 4 |
| 600 A | 1 | 2/0 |

### Equipotential bonding (10-700s)
- **10-700**: bond continuous metal water/waste-water/gas piping, conductive raised floors, livestock metal.
- **10-702** installation; **10-706** mechanically secure connections; **10-708** size: exposed ≥ No. 6 Cu / No. 4 Al; concealed/protected ≥ No. 10 Cu / No. 8 Al; or per 10-616 via 10-702 3).

## Key Takeaways
- Metering = 6-400s; service wiring = 6-300s; service-box/switch sizing is Section 8.
- Grounding (10-100s) = system/electrode; bonding (10-500s–616) = equipment; equipotential (10-700s) = non-electrical metal.
- Table 16 keys to OCPD rating — except service equipment keys to largest ungrounded ampacity (10-616 2)).
- Insulated bonding/grounding conductor in raceway with circuit/service conductors unless ≤15 m and ≤2 quarter bends.
