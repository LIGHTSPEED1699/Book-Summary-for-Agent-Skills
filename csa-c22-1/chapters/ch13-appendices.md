# Chapter 13 — Appendices A–M (pp. 524–929)

## Overview
- Appendices A–M follow the Tables/Diagrams. **Normative exceptions:** Appendix J's Introduction, Annexes J18, J20, JD and JT are **mandatory** for installations using the Class/Division system (per Rules 18-000 3)/4) and 20-000 2)/3)); Annex JB is informative. All other appendices (A, B, C, D, F, G, H, I, L, M) are explicitly **informative (non-mandatory)**.
- **Deleted in 2024 ed.:** Appendix E (TOC p. 838) and Appendix K (p. 920).

## Core Content

### Appendix A — Safety standards for electrical equipment (pp. 524–555)
- Tabulates the **C22.2 Part II product standards** behind the definition of "Approved", grouped by equipment category (e.g., batteries/secondary cells — C22.2 No. 61508-2/-3 functional safety, C61427-1, E61951-1/-2, E61959; **Wiring products** — C22.2 No. 0.3 test methods for wires/cables, 18.1–18.5 boxes/fittings/hardware, 21 cord sets, 26 wireways, 51 armoured cable AC90/ACWU90/ACG90, 96 portable/mine/wind-turbine power cables, 123 metal-sheathed cable, 124 mineral-insulated, 126.1/126.2 cable tray, 131 Type TECK 90, 159 hazardous-location wiring devices, 182.x pin-and-sleeve/special-use plugs & connectors, 184.x solid-state lighting controls, 188 splicing wire connectors, 198.1 insulating tubing).
- Use with Rule 2-100 "Approved": certify to the applicable CSA standard listed here; where none exists, other standards (e.g., ULC) apply (see Appendix B "Approved" note).

### Appendix B — Notes on Rules (pp. 556–747)

- Key Section 0 notes: **Object** — the Code's safety objectives may be met by alternative installation methods based on IEC 60364-1 fundamental safety principles, intended only for industrial/similar installations under safety-management-system or equivalent programs agreed with the AHJ; **Approved** — equipment should be certified to the CSA standards in Appendix A, or ULC standards where CSA standards don't exist/apply; **Ampacity** — see Clause C11.10 in Appendix C.

### Appendix C — Technical Committee organization and rules of procedure (pp. 748–765)
- Committee membership, subcommittees, and procedures for proposals/interpretations (Clauses C9, C12). Mostly administrative — consult for how interpretations and standards proposals are processed.
- Contains numbered explanatory clauses with technical content referenced elsewhere — e.g., **C11.10 ampacity guidance** (cited by Appendix B).
- **Annex D — Appendix A declaration checklist** (pp. 762–765, See Clauses C12.2/C12.4/C12.5): form for declaring a new/amended Part II standard compatible with the CE Code (CE Code references, definitional compatibility, conflict check vs C22.2 No. 0 horizontal standards and ORDs).

### Appendix D — Tabulated general information (pp. 765–838)
- **D1 (p. 766)** — type designations, voltage ratings, construction of wires/cables (not flexible cords); subject to frequent revision — verify against latest Part II standard.
- **D2 (783)** — DC motors; **D3 (784)** — K values for voltage drop (2/3/4 conductors, 75 °C conductor temp); **D5 (787)** — strandings; **D6 (789)/D7 (791)** — recommended tightening torques (binding screws/slotted; external drive wrenches).
- **Diagram D8–D11 + D8A/B, D9A/B, D10A/B, D11A/B (793–807)** — ampacities for cables ≤5000 V unshielded in direct-buried vs conduit/raceway installation configurations, Cu (A-series) and Al (B-series).
- **D16 (809)** — conductor sizes, fuse ratings, breaker settings for motor overload and circuit overcurrent protection (Table 26 lives here now); **D17 (816)** — ampacities for shielded cables 5–46 kV; **D18 (837)** — standard conductor sizes.
- Deleted: D4; D12A–D15B.

### Appendix F — Intrinsically safe systems descriptive documents (pp. 839–843)
- Guidelines for the **descriptive system document** required for installing/verifying intrinsically safe (IS) electrical systems and non-incendive field wiring circuits (See Rules 18-002, 18-066, J18-002, J18-066).
- Definitions: **associated apparatus** (contains IS + non-IS circuits, non-IS cannot adversely affect IS), **associated non-incendive field wiring apparatus**, **control drawing** (manufacturer document detailing interconnection electrical parameters), **IS apparatus**, **simple apparatus** (≤1.5 V, 100 mA, 25 mW, or passive component dissipating ≤1.3 W).
- F9 lists reference publications; the document is "integral to the safety of the installation".

### Appendix G — Electrical installations of fire protection systems (pp. 844–849)
- Cross-reference of CE Code Rules ↔ **National Building Code of Canada 2020 (Division B)** fire-protection electrical requirements (G3 table). Examples: Rule 2-122 ↔ NBCC 3.8.3.8 (barrier-free control mounting height); Rule 2-128 ↔ 3.1.9.1/3.1.9.3 (fire-stopping of penetrations), 3.1.13.4 (flame-spread of light diffusers), 9.10.9.6 (wiring penetrating fire separations).
- G2.2: flame-spread, vault construction etc. are covered by Code Rules 2-126, 2-128, 2-130, 2-132, 26-354. G2.3: provincial/municipal codes may deviate from NBCC — check them too.

### Appendix H — Combustible gas detection equipment (pp. 850–853)
- For use with **Rules 18-070 and J18-068**. Portable detectors: early warning, locating gas pockets, hot-work monitoring. Fixed detectors: permit non-rated equipment per Rule 18-068, verify ventilation dilution (API RP 500/505, IEC 60079-10-1), monitor process health, detect containment failure.
- Scope (H2): permanently installed **point** detectors certified to CSA C22.2 No. 60079-29-1. **Open-path detectors are NOT suitable** for 18-070/J18-068 — they give an average over the sight path, not % LFL at a specific location.
- Calibration/certification: certified for the gas group; calibrated for the target gas; multi-gas capability must read the lowest-response gas. Never a substitute for safe electrical design (sensor poisoning, maintenance, tampering risks).

### Appendix I — Interpretations (pp. 854–855)
- Committee Q&A interpretations (based on 2021-ed. numbering; current list at standardsactivities.csagroup.org). Recorded examples: Rule 4-004 7) b) iii) does **not** apply to the 4-conductor case of 4-004 3); EVSE loads under an EV energy management system may be counted per 8-106 11) — **Yes**; storage-tank water heater load included in 8-200/8-202 — **Yes**; grounding conductor connection at supply/consumer transition — **Yes** (10-118, 10-210 incl. c) transition box); concealed #10 bonding conductor in walls — **Yes** (10-708 2)); portable-generator supplementary O/C as substitute for branch-circuit O/C — **No** (14-114); storage-tank water heater is a heating device — **Yes** (62-102); bonding of rebar pads for **movable** hot tubs — **No** (68-058, three variants).

### Appendix J — Class and Division system (pp. 855–919)
- **Normative** for Division-system installations: J1 Introduction; **Annex J18** — hazardous locations classified using the Division system (mirrors Section 18 Zone rules, J18-000 Scope onward); **Annex J20** — flammable liquid/gasoline dispensing, service stations, garages, bulk storage plants, finishing processes, aircraft hangars; **Annex JB** — Notes to Rules for J18/J20 (informative).
- **Annex JD diagrams:** JD-4 open-face spray booths; JD-5 spraying outside booths; JD-6 spraying with ventilation interlocked; JD-7 tank vehicle/car loading-unloading; JD-8 outdoor pumps, vapour compressors, gas-air mixers, vaporizers; JD-9 outdoor container filling; JD-10 openings of closed spray booths/rooms.
- **Annex JT tables:** JT-63 hazardous areas for propane dispensing/container filling/storage; JT-69 hazardous locations at bulk storage plants.

### Appendix L — Hazardous area classification engineering guidelines (pp. 921–927)
- Guidance for determining hazardous areas where explosive **gas** atmospheres (oil & gas, petrochemical, refineries, distilleries) or **dust** atmospheres (food, plastics, lumber, rubber, furniture, textiles, pharma, dyes, coal/metals/fossil generation) can occur. Explicitly not a mandatory classification code; excludes catastrophic release scenarios (API RP 505 1.2.1(b)) — separate response plans required.
- L4 factor checklist (a–p): fluid properties (flash point, composition, density, vapour SG, LFL/UFL), operating P/T/flow, equipment design/maintenance, dust explosibility/confinement, housekeeping/humidity, building design, HVAC, site layout, safety systems (gas detection), terrain/topography, temperature/wind, remoteness, O&M practices, facility history, modifications.
- L5: area classification is multidisciplinary — process, HVAC, air quality, operations, fire/safety, maintenance, instrumentation; the study lead must be competent in classification principles.

## Gotchas
- Don't cite Appendix B/I/L/M content as mandatory — they are informative; the exception is the normative core of Appendix J (J18/J20/JD/JT).
- Appendix I interpretations were issued against the **2021** rule numbering — verify the rule still matches before relying on one.
- Table 26 and Tables 46/47/49/54/55 pointers mean Appendix D16 and Diagrams 1–5 are the current locations.
- Appendix H: open-path gas detectors cannot be used to justify Rule 18-068/J18-068 installations.
- Appendix G cross-references target NBCC 2020; provincial amendments may differ.
