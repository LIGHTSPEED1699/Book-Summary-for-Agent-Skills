# Chapter 11 — Occupancy-Specific Installations (Sections 66-86)

## Overview
Sections 66–86 add occupancy/environment-specific rules supplementing the general sections: temporary/event power (66, 76), water-adjacent occupancies (68, 78), factory-built and park structures (70, 72), special systems (74, 80, 84, 86). 82 is deleted.

## Core Content
- **Section 66 — Amusement parks, midways, carnivals, film/TV**
  - 66-000 temporary installs: amusement parks, midways, carnivals, fairs, film/TV/radio, concerts, trade shows.
  - 66-100 only decorative lighting/signal/comm/control circuits on ride structures; clearances 5.5/5/3.5 m.
  - 66-200/66-202 generator grounding ≥No. 4 AWG ≤50 m, bonding by cord or ≥No. 6 AWG; 66-300/66-304 per-ride/concession disconnect, lockable enclosures.
  - 66-400/66-404 hard-usage cords, polarized, mismatch-proof; Class A GFCI for 5-15R/5-20R outdoors/damp.
  - 66-450 to 66-458 single-conductor cables: ≥No. 4 AWG matched sets, O/C >10 000 A (66-452), free-air ampacity (66-454), colour-coded locking connectors, caps, >150 V-to-ground labelling/enclosures (66-456), bonding every circuit (66-458).

- **Section 68 — Pools, tubs, spas (GFCI organizing theme)**
  - 68-000 scope: permanent/storable pools, hydromassage tubs, spas, wading/baptismal/decorative pools, splash pads; metal within 3 m incl.
  - 68-052 to 68-056 no wiring in pool walls/water except as permitted; no overhead within 5.0 m of edge (≤50 kV at 7.5 m); underground per Table 61.
  - 68-058 bonding copper ≥No. 6 AWG: rebar ≥4 perimeter connections or copper grid 1.5 m out; ≥58 cm² water-contact surface if nothing bonded touches water.
  - 68-064 no receptacles within 1.5 m, Class A GFCI at 1.5–3 m; 68-066 wet-niche luminaires ≤600 mm submersion, ≤150 V, GFCI below/within 3 m.
  - 68-068 Class A GFCIs permanently connected, ≥3 m from water; required for in-water equipment, spas/hot tubs, unbarriered equipment within 3 m, wet-area receptacles.
  - 68-072 disconnects ≥1.5 m or barriered, lockable if unseen; 68-202 pump receptacle 1.5–7.5 m; 68-302 to 68-308 hydromassage GFCI + controls ≥1 m; 68-402 to 68-408 spas: emergency shut-off ≤15 m w/ alarms, blowers above rim.

- **Section 70 — Factory-built relocatable/non-relocatable structures**
  - 70-000 relocatable (mobile homes etc.) Rules 70-100–70-130; non-relocatable factory-built Rules 70-200–70-204; RVs excluded (Z240 RV Series).
  - 70-108 extra-hard-usage cords ≥7.5 m; 70-110 main O/C ≥ ampacity, min 50 A mobile homes; 70-112 accessible, not in closets/baths; 70-114 nail plates; 70-126 chassis bonding per Table 41; 70-128/70-130 marking + factory tests.
  - 70-200 non-relocatable also follow 70-100, 70-112, 70-114, 70-118, 70-122 to 70-130.

- **Section 72 — Mobile home and RV parks**
  - 72-102 RV lot demand: 12 000 W/14-50R, 3600 W/5-30R or TT-30R, 2400 W/5-20R, 1800 W/5-15R, 600 W/tenting receptacle.
  - 72-104/72-106 individual O/C + accessible disconnect per lot; 72-108 receptacles 5-15R/5-20R/TT-30R/14-50R, 5-15R/5-20R → Class A GFCI.
  - 72-112 supply equipment driver's side, 600 mm–1.2 m above ground.

- **Section 74 — Airport series lighting circuits**
  - 74-004 Type ASLC cable buried ≥450 mm, concrete-encased raceway ≥600 mm under runways/roads; separations 75/300 mm.
  - 74-006 direct-burial isolating transformers ≥450 mm, polarized grounded secondary; 74-008/74-010 counterpoise copper ≥No. 8 AWG bonded to stakes/transformers/electrodes/housings.

- **Section 76 — Temporary wiring (construction, Appendix G)**
  - 76-002/76-004 Section 12 conductors or extra-hard-usage outdoor cord, grounding per Section 10; 76-006 service equipment authorized-access, lockable, ≤200 A.
  - 76-010 armoured-cable feeders; 76-012 lighting separate from power, ≤80% of breaker per 30-104.
  - 76-014 no interconnection with permanent circuits except by special permission; 76-016 construction 5-15R/5-20R → Class A GFCI.

- **Section 78 — Marine wharves, docking facilities**
  - 78-050 receptacle types by ampacity (pin-and-sleeve >60 A); weatherproof, above max water level; 125 V 15/20 A → Class A GFCI.
  - 78-052 individual branch circuit per shore-power receptacle; feeder GFP ≤30 mA; 78-054 demand factors declining 100%→10%.
  - 78-056 corrosion-resistant raceways/NMWU/MI/armoured, flexible cord for tides; 78-058 bonding ≥No. 12 AWG salt spray, underwater grounding grid where shore electrodes impractical; 78-062 gasoline dispensing per Section 20, grade = highest water surface.

- **Section 80 — Cathodic protection**
  - 80-002 DC wiring per Section 12, buried ≥450/200 mm; hazardous areas per Sections 18/20.
  - 80-004 conductors ≥No. 12 AWG; 80-006 below-grade splices welded/compression + sealed, piping thermit-welded/anodic clamps.
  - 80-010 disconnect within sight and 15 m of rectifier; 80-012 >50 V: ≤10 V earth gradient at 1 m; 80-014 warning tags, bypass sign, drawings.

- **Section 84 — Interconnection of power production sources**
  - 84-002 interconnection per the supply authority's requirements; 84-004 back-feed protection; 84-006 synchronization.
  - 84-008 loss of supply voltage → auto-disconnect until restored (inverter may serve); 84-010/84-012 overcurrent both directions (transformer O/C per Section 26); 84-016 GFP per 14-102.
  - 84-020/84-022/84-024/84-026 disconnects both sides, lockable, isolating means; 84-028 grounding per Sections 10/36; 84-030 warning + single-line diagram.

- **Section 86 — Electric vehicle charging systems**
  - 86-100 EVSE = cables, connectors, devices for power/information transfer between circuit and EV.
  - 86-102 ≤1000 V ac nominal, ≤1060 V dc (1500 V dc qualified-only, DANGER-marked).
  - 86-300 separate branch circuit (shared OK w/ EV energy management per 8-106 10)/11); 86-302 continuous load per 8-104.
  - 86-304 disconnect >60 A or 150 V-to-ground, lockable open; 86-306 labelled receptacles, outdoor 5-20R within 2.5 m → Class A GFCI.
  - 86-308 V2G marked (Section 84 applies); 86-400 indoor ventilation per 64-802 interlocked w/ EVSE; 86-402 outdoor; 86-404 hazardous locations.

- **Section 82 — Deleted** in C22.1:24 (no requirements).

## Key Takeaways
- Class A GFCI is the recurring safety gate: events (66-404), pools/spas (68-064, 68-066, 68-068), RV lots (72-108 4)), construction (76-016), marinas (78-050 5)), outdoor EV (86-306 2)); clearances carry the risk analysis: pool 1.5 m/3 m zones, 5.0/7.5 m overhead, 15 m disconnect sight rules.
- Bonding is engineered: pool grids (68-058), show circuits (66-456/66-458), salt-spray No. 12 AWG (78-058); temporary/permanent stay separate (76-014); interconnection follows the supply authority (84-002); EVSE continuous load (86-302), disconnects >60 A / 150 V-to-ground (86-304).
