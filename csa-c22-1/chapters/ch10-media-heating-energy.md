# Chapter 10 — Communications, Heating, Renewables & Energy Storage (Sections 52–64)

## Overview

Sections 52–64 — diagnostic imaging, CATV/radio-TV, optical fiber, ropeways, communication systems, fixed electric heating, renewables/ESS/batteries. S64 carries the most 2024 revisions (Δ).

## Core Content

### Section 52 — Diagnostic imaging installations
- Momentary vs long-time ratings; 52-004 — guarding of high-voltage parts; 52-006 — supply connection; 52-016 (Δ) — ampacity and overcurrent protection.

### Section 54 — Community antenna distribution and radio/TV
- 54-000–54-010 — scope/terminology (CATV = community antenna distribution); 54-100/54-102 — conductors, voltage limits; 54-104/106/108 — circuits.
- 54-200 — grounding of coax outer shield; 54-300s — installation; 54-400s — separation from light and power circuits.

### Section 56 — Optical fiber cables
- 56-102 — cable types: nonconductive, conductive, hybrid (metallic members); 56-200s — raceway/enclosure requirements.

### Section 58 — Passenger ropeways and similar equipment
- 58-012 — grounding/bonding; 58-014 — voltage limits (48 V signals; 750 V motive); 58-016 — luminaires.

### Section 60 — Electrical communication systems
- 60-000 — applies to communication systems; supplements/amends general rules. 60-100 — "Exposed plant": lightning, accidental power contact (>300 V rms), induction, ground potential rise; 60-102 (Δ) — Rule 2-024 exemption if not connected to exposed plant/telecom network.
- 60-104 (Δ) — circuits in communication cables; 60-106/108; 60-200s protection; 60-300s installation; 60-704 grounding.

### Section 62 — Fixed electric heating
- 62-000 — scope: space/surface/other fixed heating; 62-102 (Δ) — terminology: bare element water heater, central unit, trace heater.
- 62-110 — branch circuits; 62-112 — combustibles (90 °C); 62-114 — overcurrent; 62-206 — central units: disconnect within sight, ≤9 m.
- 62-208 — trace heater/panel locations; 62-218 — slab heating: 50 mm spacing, 40 °C ambient.

### Section 64 — Renewables, energy production, ESS, and batteries (Δ-heavy)

**General (64-050s)**
- 64-050 — renewable/ESS wiring may supply a building alongside other services; 64-052 — separate conductors of different systems (12-904 2), 12-3030); 64-054 — common return ampacity ≥ sum of supply-circuit OCPD ratings.
- 64-056 — bipolar systems: separate raceways/enclosures per monopole; warning re overvoltage on grounded-conductor disconnection; 64-058 — overcurrent from all sources, dc-marked OCPDs, backfeed suitability.
- 64-060 (Δ) — disconnects: all ungrounded conductors, load-break, lockable, dual-energization warning; interconnected per S84; equipment disconnect within sight + 3 m or lockable. 64-062 (Δ) — isolating means as equipment disconnect if ≤30 A with separate disconnect elsewhere.
- 64-064 — indoor dc conductors in metal raceway/armour/sheath to first accessible disconnect; 64-066 — one conductor of 2-wire dc circuits grounded (functional grounding OK for PV); ground-fault protection detects/indicates/controls.

**Solar PV (64-200s)**
- 64-200 — disconnect marking: operating V/I, max PV voltage (64-202), Isc; rapid-shutdown marking; warning sign 9.5 mm white on red; 64-204 — voltage-drop options incl. ≤5% of rated voltage.
- 64-202 — max voltage = Voc × 125% or temperature-coefficient method; dwellings ≤600 V dc, metal raceway above 30 V; (Δ) ≤1500 V dc exempt from Section 36 rules for qualified-only inaccessible installs with DANGER marking.
- 64-206 — ampacity = lesser of OCPD rating or conductor ampacity, ≥125% Isc; 64-208 — application class: A/C public areas OK, B not.
- 64-210 (Δ) — array wiring: extra-hard flexible cords, module cables (≤30 V, or >30 V if not readily accessible), RPVU cable, supports 300 mm from boxes/≤1 m; rodent protection absent integral dc arc-fault protection; RPVU in raceway indoors; NBC flame spread.

**Wind (64-400s)**
- 64-400 — tower-base marking: OCPD values, interrupting capacity, generator type, output, 84-030 warning.
- 64-402 — conductors ≥125% max generator current, ≥90 °C; 64-404 — OCPDs ≤125%, accessible; 64-406 — disconnect at tower base, rated 125% (nameplate exception, large turbines), labelled.
- 64-408 — towers bonded ≥No. 2/0 AWG bare copper; electrodes interconnected (10-104); station electrodes within 2.4 m interconnected. 64-410 — maintenance receptacles need Class A GFCI; 64-412 — lightning electrodes bonded to tower system.

**Micro-hydropower (64-500s)**
- 64-500 — marking: description, rated power, ballast V/I; 64-502 — conductors ≥125% full load, ≥90 °C; 64-504 — S12 wiring, S22 moist locations.
- 64-506 (Δ) — OCPD not required where fault current ≤ ampacity; ≤12 A may use 15 A device; 64-510 — stand-alone: governor rated to installed capacity, diversion load ≥100% of governor rating.

**Batteries/ESS (64-800s)**
- 64-800 — batteries per 64-802–64-814 (manufacturer instructions govern); associated equipment per 64-816–820.
- 64-802 — ventilate gassing batteries; lead-acid ≤45 °C, above electrolyte freezing; 64-804 — exposed energized parts authorized-only, dwellings ≤50 V dc guarded, trays insulated ≥1500 V, corrosion-protected, no conductors within 150 mm of lead-acid case tops.
- 64-806 — current-limiting OCPD when battery fault current exceeds equipment ratings; 64-808 — strings >50 V dc: disconnect strings and grounded conductors separately; 64-810 (Δ) — bonding per Section 10.
- 64-812 (Δ) — fine-strand conductors at terminals, busbars OK, no flexible metal raceway, secured ≤1.5 m; 64-814 (Δ) — battery-to-equipment conductors ≥2/0 AWG, ≥300 mm at terminals, >3 m per Section 12 with 14-100 protection; corrosive electrolyte: sealed corrosion-resistant raceway/cable ≥300 mm above highest terminal. 64-816 — non-battery battery-room wiring as dry location.
- 64-818 (Δ) — charge control required; exemption: renewable-only charging, V/I within battery limits, (charge current × 1 h) <3% of capacity, qualified-only adjustments; 64-820 — sole diversion controller requires additional independent overcharge prevention.

## Key Takeaways

- The 125% multiplier recurs: PV voltage/ampacity (64-202/206), wind/hydro conductors/OCPDs (64-402s, 64-502/506). Disconnects: accessible, lockable, labelled, dual-source warnings (64-060, 64-200, 64-400); indoor dc needs metal containment (64-064, 64-202 4)).
- Batteries: ventilation, current-limiting OCPD, fine-strand conductors, charge control with narrow renewable-only exemption (64-802–64-818); S54/56/60: separation from power, shield grounding, exposed-plant protection.
