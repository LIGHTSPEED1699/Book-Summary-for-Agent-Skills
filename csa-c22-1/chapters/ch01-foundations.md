# Chapter 1 — Foundations: Object, Scope, Definitions, General Rules (C22.1:24)

Source: CSA C22.1:24 Section 0 (pp. 53–67) and Section 2 (pp. 68–75). Distilled — consult the Code for full rule text.

## Overview
- **Section 0 has no rule numbers in 2024**: Object/Scope are unnumbered headings; definitions are alphabetical entries. There is **no Section 1** (merged into Section 0).
- **Section 2 "General Rules"** = administrative backbone: permits, approval, workmanship, protection of persons, maintenance/working space, enclosures.
- **Δ marker** = new or significantly changed in 2024 (e.g., 2-116, 2-328, 8-500).

## Core Content

### Section 0 — Object and Scope
- Object: safety standards for **installation and maintenance**; prevent **fire and shock**. Grounded in **IEC 60364-1** principles (shock, thermal, overcurrent, fault current, overvoltage); alternatives permitted only if the AHJ assesses them as meeting those principles.
- **Not a design specification** or manual for untrained persons. Scope: all electrical work **at all voltages** in buildings, premises, factory-built structures, marine vessels >5 months on shore supply.
- **Exceptions:** utility function (electric/communication/CATV), electric-railway motive power, outdoor railway signalling, aircraft, Transport Canada ships; mines/quarries → CSA M421.

### Section 0 — Key Definitions (alphabetical; cite as "Section 0 — <term>")
- **Ampacity** — max current (A) carried continuously without exceeding temperature limits.
- **Approved** — acceptable to the inspection department (certified by accredited organization).
- **Branch circuit** — wiring between final overcurrent device and outlet(s); **Feeder** — between service box (or source) and branch-circuit overcurrent devices.
- **Circuit breaker** — non-automatic open/close + automatic trip on overcurrent, without damage to itself. **Conductor** — bare / covered (dielectric, no rated strength) / insulated (rated dielectric strength).
- **Energized / Dead / Dead front** — is a source of voltage / free of voltage & charge / no live parts exposed on operating side.
- **Disconnecting means** — device(s) disconnecting circuit conductors from their source of supply.
- **Dwelling unit (Δ)** — housekeeping suite with cooking, eating, living, sleeping, sanitary facilities.
- **Exposed (live parts)** — touchable or approachable nearer than a safe distance; not guarded/isolated/insulated. **Exposed (wiring)** — not concealed; **Concealed** — permanently inaccessible by structure/finish.
- **Ground fault** — unintentional path from a normally-at-potential part to ground. **Class A GFCI** — trips at ≥6 mA, not at ≤4 mA.
- **Grounded** — effectively connected to the mass of earth (low impedance, adequate ampacity); **grounding conductor** connects service/system to the **grounding electrode** (buried pipe/object/device).
- **Guarded** — covered/shielded/fenced or protected by location. **Hazardous location** — ignitable gases/vapours/dusts/fibres needing special precautions.
- **Identified** — recognizable as suitable for a specific purpose (marking/colour).
- **Location** — damp (protected but moisture-prone), dry, wet (weather/washdown), ordinary, hazardous.
- **Service box** — lockable/sealable enclosure containing service conductors.

### Section 2 — Administration (2-000 to 2-034)
- **2-000** Inspection department administers the Code. **2-004–2-012** Permit, application, fees; post permit; notify inspector. **2-014** Plans/specs where required. **2-016** Current-permit = permission to the supply authority to energize. **2-018/2-020** Reconnection requires authorization; reinspection on demand.
- **2-024** All equipment shall be **approved**; **2-026** powers of rejection; **2-030** AHJ may permit deviation (referenced by 8-106 5)); **2-028/2-032/2-034** work available for inspection; no damage/interference.

### Section 2 — Technical (2-100 to 2-140)
- **2-100/2-102** Marking; warning/caution markings in locally mandated language(s).
- **2-104** SCCR/withstand ≥ available fault current; dual-rated equipment (120/240, 208Y/120, 480Y/277, 600Y/347 V) only on **solidly grounded** systems — V-to-ground ≤ lower rating, line-to-line ≤ higher.
- **2-106** Rebuilt equipment: new nameplate; **rebuilt moulded-case breakers are not approved**. **2-108** Substitute larger size/rating if exact unprocurable (smaller only via 2-030).
- **2-110** Dwelling branch circuits ≤ **150 V-to-ground**; exception: apartments >250 kV·A service + qualified maintenance may use up to **347 V-to-ground** for fixed space heating (thermostats ≤300 V-to-ground), water heating, A/C.
- **2-112/2-114/2-116 (Δ)/2-120** Quality of work; no wood anchors into masonry/concrete; corrosion protection, avoid dissimilar metals; AWG = **copper** AWG unless stated.
- **2-122/2-124** Access to nameplates/maintainable parts; no dangerous proximity of non-electrical equipment. **2-126** Thermal insulation: mind conductor temp in insulated cavities; 25 mm separation from metal-faced insulation (knob-and-tube); NM cable may contact batts; never spray insulation into boxes.
- **2-128/2-130/2-132/2-134** Fire spread — seal penetrations per NBC; flame spread (wiring, cables, raceways) per NBC; seismic restraint per NBC where required.
- **2-136** Sunlight resistance marking (protect inner conductors where jacket removed at termination). **2-138** Completed wiring free from short circuits and grounds (except Section 10).
- **2-140** Class A GFCI = supplementary protection only; not a substitute for insulation/grounding (except 26-702 2)).

### Section 2 — Protection of Persons (2-200 to 2-202)
- **2-200** Install/guard for safety of persons/property and against mechanical damage.
- **2-202** Guard bare live parts (enclosures or qualified-only rooms); barriers for serviceable non-electrical components within **900 mm** of bare live parts; warning signs forbidding unqualified entry.

### Section 2 — Maintenance and Operation (2-300 to 2-328)
- **2-300** Keep equipment safe; emergency equipment periodically tested; defective equipment repaired or permanently disconnected. **2-302** Hazardous locations per Rule 18-010.
- **2-304** No live repairs unless disconnection infeasible; 3-/4-way switches are **not** disconnecting means; locks/notices/sentries against re-energization.
- **2-306** Arc-flash/shock field marking (switchboards, panelboards, industrial control panels, meter sockets, MCCs — non-dwelling), visible before servicing; **Δ 3)** OCPD ≥ **1200 A**: reduce arcing fault clearing time + documentation.
- **2-308** Working space ≥ **1 m**, secure footing (doors ≥90°); Table 56 for exposed live parts; headroom **2.2 m**. **2-310** Egress; ≥1200 A or >750 V rooms: exit without passing failure point, else space ≥ **1.5 m**; doors openable without key/tool.
- **2-312** Transformers >50 kV·A: 1 m on connection-access sides. **2-314** No storage in working space. **2-316** Rooftop HVAC receptacle (per 26-710). **2-318** Mobile structures: 5-15R/5-20R via transfer equipment.
- **2-320/2-322/2-324/2-326** Illumination; no flammable storage near equipment; ventilation; unimpeded drainage. **2-328 (Δ)** Clearances to flammable-gas equipment per **CSA B149.1/B149.2**.

### Section 2 — Enclosures (2-400 to 2-404)
- **2-400** Enclosure/box types and use — **Table 65**. **2-402** Ingress protection per **IP Code**. **2-404** Motor enclosure marking.

## Key Takeaways
- Scope is **all voltages**; the utility/rail/aircraft/ship exceptions are narrow and enumerated.
- "Approved," deviations, reconnection all route through the **AHJ** — 2-024 and 2-030 are the hinges.
- **2-104** dual-voltage/fault-rating limits quietly control equipment selection on 347/600 V systems.
- Working-space numbers (1 m / 1.5 m / 2.2 m; Table 56) and the 1200 A arc-flash rule (**2-306 3)**) are inspection-critical.
- Section 0 definitions have **no rule numbers** — cite as "Section 0 — <term>".
