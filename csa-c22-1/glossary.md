# CSA C22.1:24 Glossary — Key Definitions (Section 0)

Verified verbatim-gists from the definitions in Section 0 (source pp. 53-67). When precision matters, read the full definition in the source — some entries have subclauses.

## Core electrical terms

- **Ampacity** — the maximum current, in amperes, that can be carried continuously under the conditions of use a) by a conductor without exceeding its temperature rating; or b) by an insulated conductor without exceeding the insulation temperature rating.
- **Approved** — equipment certified by an SCC-accredited certification organization, or conforming to the regulatory authority's requirements.
- **Bonding** — a low impedance path obtained by permanently joining all non-current-carrying metal parts to ensure electrical continuity and having the capacity to conduct safely any current likely to be imposed on it.
- **Bonding conductor** — a conductor connecting non-current-carrying parts of equipment/raceways/enclosures to the service equipment or system grounding conductor.
- **Grounding** — a permanent and continuous conductive path to earth with sufficient ampacity for fault current and sufficiently low impedance to limit voltage rise and facilitate protective-device operation.
- **Grounding conductor** — the conductor connecting the service equipment or system to the grounding electrode.
- **Grounding electrode** — a buried metal water-piping system or metal object/device buried in or driven into the ground, connected to the grounding conductor.
- **Grounded** — effectively connected to the general mass of earth via a low-impedance grounding path of adequate ampacity (full definition has subclauses a/b).
- **Ground fault** — an unintentional electrical path between a part normally at some potential to ground, and ground.
- **GFCI** — interrupts a circuit when current to ground exceeds a predetermined value below the OCPD operating threshold. **Class A GFCI**: trips at ≥6 mA ground fault, does not trip at ≤4 mA.
- **Ground fault protection** — detection + interruption of ground fault current below the circuit OCPD threshold.
- **Neutral** — the conductor (when one exists) of a polyphase or single-phase 3-wire circuit intended to have voltage differences approximately equal in magnitude and equally spaced in phase to the other conductors.
- **Identified** — for a conductor: white covering or raised ridge = grounded/neutral conductor; for terminals: tinned/nickel-plated/marked for neutral connection.
- **Insulated** — separated from other conducting surfaces by a dielectric or air space with resistance high enough for the condition of use.
- **Overcurrent device** — device that automatically opens a circuit under BOTH overload and short-circuit conditions (fusing or electromechanical).
- **Overload device** — protects from excess current but not necessarily short-circuit; auto-opens the circuit.

## Circuit taxonomy (Section 0 — "Circuit")

- **Branch circuit** — between the final overcurrent device and the outlet(s).
- **Feeder** — between the service box (or other source of supply) and the branch-circuit overcurrent devices.
- **Consumer's service** — all of the consumer's installation from the service box (or equivalent) up to the supply service.
- **Supply service** — conductors run by the supply authority from its mains to the consumer's service.
- **Multi-wire branch circuit** — two or more ungrounded conductors with voltage between them + identified grounded conductor equally spaced; grounded conductor connected to the neutral.
- **Control circuit** — carries signals directing a control device; does not carry the device's power.
- **Remote control circuit** — controls another circuit through a relay or equivalent.
- **Signal circuit** — carries signals/information (not power).
- **Low-energy power circuit** — power limited per Class 2 remote-control requirements.
- **Extra-low-voltage power circuit** — ≤30 V from a source restricted to 1000 V·A, current not Class 2-limited (e.g., valve operators).

## Locations

- **Dry location** — not normally subject to dampness (temporary construction-site dampness acceptable with adequate ventilation).
- **Damp location** — normally or periodically subject to condensation of moisture in/on/adjacent to equipment; includes partially protected locations under canopies, marquees, roofed open porches.
- **Wet location** — liquids may drip, splash, or flow on or against electrical equipment.
- **Ordinary location** — a dry location not unduly exposed to mechanical damage, dust, moisture, extreme temperatures; free from corrosive/flammable/explosive atmospheres.
- **Outdoor location** — any location exposed to the weather.
- **Hazardous location** — premises/parts where an explosive gas atmosphere or hazardous dusts are or may be present in quantities requiring special precautions (see Section 18/Appendix B).

## Equipment & wiring

- **Raceway** — any channel for holding wires/cables/busbars: conduit (rigid/flexible, metal/non-metallic), EMT/RNMC, underfloor raceways, cellular floors, surface raceways, wireways, busways, auxiliary gutters.
- **Receptacle** — one or more groups of female contacts on the same yoke/housing at an outlet, for attachment plugs. Duplex (2 groups), single (1), split (2+ with multi-wire terminals).
- **Outlet** — a point where current is taken to supply utilization equipment.
- **Panelboard** — assembly of buses, overcurrent devices, control apparatus, with/without switches, in a cabinet.
- **Switchboard** — panel(s) with switching/measuring/control/protective devices and buses; designed to carry and rupture maximum fault current.
- **Service box** — lockable/sealable enclosure containing fuses + switch, or a circuit breaker, operable manually when closed.
- **Grounding electrode / grounding conductor** — see core terms above.
- **Busway** — metal trough raceway containing conductors supported on insulators. **Cablebus** — assembly of insulated conductors in a ventilated/non-ventilated metal housing.
- **Cable tray** — troughing + fittings allowing install/removal of cables after installation (ladder / ventilated / non-ventilated variants).
- **Mineral-insulated cable (MI/LWMI)** — bare solid conductors insulated by compressed refractory material in a liquid- and gas-tight metal tube sheath.
- **Luminaire** — complete lighting unit designed to accommodate lamp(s) and connect to circuit conductors.

## People, permits, and administration

- **Qualified person** — one familiar with the construction and operation of the apparatus and the hazards involved.
- **Authorized person** — a qualified person obliged to approach/handle equipment in their duties, or one warned and instructed/authorized by someone with authority.
- **Inspection department** — organization legally authorized to enforce the Code for a territory. **Inspector** — person appointed by it. **Permit** — official written permission to commence work. **Special permission** — written authority of the inspection department.
- **Accredited certification organization** — accredited by the Standards Council of Canada to run an electrical equipment certification program.

## Occupancy & buildings

- **Single dwelling** — detached house, one row-housing unit, or one unit of a semi-detached/duplex/triplex/quadruplex house.
- **Suite** — single room(s) of complementary use under a single tenancy (incl. dwelling units, hotel/motel guest rooms).
- **Residential occupancy** — occupancy providing sleeping accommodation (excluding medical care/detention cases).
- **Industrial establishment** — building/part where persons assemble, fabricate, manufacture, process, repair, or store goods (vs. dwellings/offices).
- **Bathroom** — a room containing bathing or showering facilities; may also contain wash basin(s) and/or water closet(s). (Drives GFCI rules, 26-704.)
- **Recreational vehicle / RV park / Park model trailer / Relocatable structure / Mobile home** — see source for each (drives Sections 70/72).
- **Plenum** — chamber for distributing/receiving processed air (drives wiring-method restrictions, Section 12/2-1xx).
- **Readily accessible** — reachable quickly for operation/renewal/inspection without climbing over/removing obstacles or using portable ladders.
- **Special permission** — written authority of the inspection department.
- **Supply authority** — organization responsible for the power distribution network connecting to the consumer's service.

## Voltage classes

- **High-voltage / Low-voltage / Extra-low voltage** — see "Voltage" definition cluster in Section 0; Section 36 applies above 750 V (referenced from Tables section headings). Extra-low-voltage power circuits: ≤30 V, ≤1000 V·A source.
- **Low-voltage protection** — operates on voltage reduction/failure to interrupt power AND maintain interruption. **Low-voltage release** — interrupts but permits re-establishment when voltage returns.
